**Date:** 2026-09-08

**Purpose:** convert broad case studies into bounded archival work.

## London

### Working Men's College institutional archive

National Archives Discovery records the WMC collection as:

- **Reference:** `LMA/4535`
- **Coverage:** 1842-1997
- **Description:** records including student register, correspondence, scrapbooks, etc.
- Discovery route: https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/c/F157579

Highest-yield nineteenth-century questions:

1. College Museum foundation/contributions from 1856: donors, accession lists, cabinets, specimens, curator/secretary records.
2. Mordecai C. Cooke 1861 Physiological Botany: class register, pupils, fees, room, apparatus, microscope/preparations.
3. Alfred Grugeon: transition pupil/associate -> WMC Botany teacher.
4. Harry Govier Seeley: museum secretaryship and any duties/listings.
5. Thomas Davies: identify exact years/classes of evening attendance if student registers permit.
6. Lubbock Field Club from 1893: microscopic observations, specimen exhibitions, apparatus or slide ownership.

### Quekett archive / slide collection

Priority window: **1865-1875**.

Crosswalk early WMC actors against:
- membership lists;
- minute books;
- slide donations/acquisitions;
- loan/collection catalogues;
- exchange notices;
- makers' labels.

Goal: close `named WMC actor -> named physical preparation/slide transaction`.

## Sheffield

### People's College Sheffield Archive

University of Sheffield Special Collections:
- **Collection ref:** `10`
- **Coverage:** 1837-1912
- **Extent:** 1 box; item-level catalogue available.
- Main route: https://archives.shef.ac.uk/repositories/3/resources/386

#### Highest-priority exact items

**`10/4/1/9` — John Derby notebooks, 1842-1845**

Catalogue includes:
- German grammar;
- Greek grammar;
- history;
- a notebook titled **`Humanity`**, explicitly catalogued as referring to **human biology**;
- languages and general notebooks.

Route: https://archives.shef.ac.uk/repositories/3/archival_objects/27016

Research questions:
- what biological/anatomical content was taught;
- diagrams, specimens, demonstration language;
- references to practical observation, lenses, glass, preparations or collecting;
- teacher/source-text identity.

**`10/4/1/7` — Syllabus of examination, 8 Aug 1844**

Route: https://archives.shef.ac.uk/repositories/3/archival_objects/27014

Use to reconstruct actual examinable subject structure rather than relying on retrospective curriculum lists.

**`10/2/7` — The People's College Journal, 1846-1847**

Two surviving issues; search for science teaching, natural-history objects, demonstrations, excursions, apparatus, museums and student science.

Route: https://archives.shef.ac.uk/repositories/3/archival_objects/26999

**`10/4/2` — John Derby correspondence, notes and papers, 1842-1878**

32 items. Search later institutional memory, teachers, class organization, purchases, student careers and post-Bayley continuity.

Route: https://archives.shef.ac.uk/repositories/3/archival_objects/27022

**Annual reports / Box 1**

The catalogue includes the First Annual Report (1843) and related People's College records. Use reports to reconstruct enrolment, subjects, teachers, finance and any apparatus/object donations.

Container route: https://archives.shef.ac.uk/repositories/3/top_containers/2244

## Cambridge

### Josiah Chater diaries — Museum of Cambridge

The diaries are held by the Museum of Cambridge and are being progressively transcribed through Capturing Cambridge.

Routes:
- https://www.museumofcambridge.org.uk/2022/07/josiah-chaters-diaries-a-glimpse-back-in-time/
- https://capturingcambridge.org/projects/diary-josiah-chater-cambridge-victorian/

The site already indexes 1855 and 1856, but published excerpts are incomplete.

Priority full-diary window: **May 1855-c.1863**, then extend to later Working Men's Club/Institute activity.

Search/record:
- `Working College`, `College`, teacher surnames;
- science/natural philosophy/chemistry/botany;
- lecture, demonstration, experiment;
- microscope, glass, slide, object, specimen, preparation, cabinet;
- museum/exhibition/conversazione;
- fees, books, materials;
- work hours and scheduling constraints.

### Cambridge Working Men's College -> Free Library custodial lead

Trinity College Cambridge:
- **Add. MS c/104/21**
- Robert Bowes to Nora Sidgwick, 13 Apr. 1904.

The catalogue records that Canon Gray, successor to Archdeacon Vesey as secretary of the Cambridge WMC, identified Sidgwick's election in October 1860 and promised to search for further material and send relevant items to Cambridge to be placed in the **Free Library**.

Route: https://archives.trin.cam.ac.uk/index.php/informationobject/browse?names=63206&sf_culture=en&sort=lastUpdated&sortDir=desc&topLod=0

Research task:
1. determine the successor custody of Cambridge Free Library local-history/manuscript collections;
2. search accession catalogues around 1904-1905 for Gray/Bowes/Vesey/WMC;
3. locate prospectuses, minutes, teacher lists or other papers deposited through this route.

## Comparative archival rule

For all three cities, extract four ledgers separately:

1. **people** — student, teacher, curator, preparer, dealer;
2. **objects** — specimen, slide, preparation, cabinet, instrument;
3. **transactions** — donated, bought, borrowed, mounted, lent, exchanged, catalogued;
4. **skills** — observation, preparation, drawing, cataloguing, language, calculation.

Only build city-to-city or institution-to-microscopy edges after at least two ledgers meet in a dated source.

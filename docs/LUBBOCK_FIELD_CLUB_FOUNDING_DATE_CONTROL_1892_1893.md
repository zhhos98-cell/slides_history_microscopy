# Lubbock Field Club founding-date control — 1892 / 1893

**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Branch:** `research/working-mens-microscopy-19c`  
**Status:** evidence reweighted after direct reading of Ramsbottom 1917. **1893 is now the strongest working date**, while the 1892 conflict remains open pending direct inspection of the malformed 1904 page and contemporary 1892–93 material.

## 1. Why this needs a separate control

Several sources have supplied incompatible dates for formation of the later WMC **Lubbock Field Club**. The conflict should not be silently normalized because it affects the chronology of Alfred Grugeon's retirement, presidency, College Journal documentation, and the relation between older WMC botanical-walk traditions and the new club.

## 2. Evidence for 1893 — now strongest

### John Ramsbottom, 1917

In his direct biographical notice `Alfred Grugeon (1826–1913)` in *Journal of Botany* 55 (1917): 193–94, Ramsbottom says Grugeon was president of the Lubbock Field Club **from its foundation in 1893 until his death**.

This is retrospective, but Ramsbottom explicitly says most facts were derived from:

- Grugeon's own unpublished `Botanical Reminiscences`, read before the Lubbock Field Club in 1896;
- *The Working Men's College Journal* for 1913;
- further information supplied by C. E. Britton.

Thus Ramsbottom's 1893 statement is unusually well-provenanced and close to an actor-level retrospective source.

Evidence state:

`1893_RAMSBOTTOM = STRONG_RETROSPECTIVE_ACTOR_DERIVED`

### Marcella Pellegrino Sutcliffe, 2014

Sutcliffe independently describes the Lubbock Field Club as established in **1893** and cites exact WMC/LMA archival reference:

`4535/E/02/06/001`

for the Club's natural-science aims.

The exact item type/date has not yet been independently recovered from the public catalogue, but this provides a separate archive-based route to 1893.

Evidence state:

`1893_SUTCLIFFE = STRONG_ARCHIVAL_REFERENCE_ROUTE`

## 3. Evidence for 1892 — retain, but downgraded

### Peter Ayres

Ayres says Alfred Grugeon retired from WMC teaching in **1892**, when he became President of the `newly formed Lubbock Field Club`.

This may conflate retirement and club formation, or may preserve a real late-1892 organizational stage. It remains useful but is no longer the strongest date witness.

Evidence state:

`1892_AYRES = SECONDARY_CHRONOLOGY_CONFLICT`

### 1904 WMC self-history OCR

The Internet Archive OCR of R. H. Marks's `The College Clubs` reads in sequence:

- `In 1890 we have records in the College Journals of botanical walks ...`;
- `In March of the following year, 1892, the present Lubbock Field Club was formed ...`

The grammar is internally impossible: the following year after 1890 is 1891.

Possible explanations:

1. OCR misread 1891 as 1890;
2. OCR misread the formation year;
3. printed source contains a typographical error;
4. line/page layout has confused OCR sequencing.

Evidence state:

`1904_OCR_DATE = MALFORMED / PAGE_IMAGE_REQUIRED`

Do not cite `March 1892` as a settled primary date until the printed page is inspected.

## 4. Current evidence weighting

### Preferred working date

`LUBBOCK_FIELD_CLUB_FOUNDATION = 1893_STRONGLY_FAVORED`

Reasons:

- Ramsbottom gives 1893 directly and identifies actor-level source material behind his biography;
- Sutcliffe independently gives 1893 through a WMC archival reference;
- the main 1892 support is a modern chronology plus malformed OCR from the 1904 institutional history.

### But not fully closed

Retain:

`1892_CONFLICT = OPEN`

until at least one of the following is obtained:

1. direct image/physical reading of the 1904 page;
2. direct WMC Journal formation notice;
3. LMA `4535/E/02/06/001` item date/title;
4. contemporary local/scientific press notice from 1892–93.

## 5. Possible reconciliation models to test

Do not select one without evidence:

- late-1892 informal organization / presidency, formal club establishment 1893;
- Grugeon retired in 1892 but presidency began 1893;
- Ayres rounded the formation to retirement year;
- 1904 printing/OCR anomaly;
- separate formation meeting and rules/adoption dates.

## 6. Periodical implications

Search windows should cover **both 1892 and 1893**, not just the preferred 1893 date.

Targets:

- WMC Journal: `Lubbock Field Club`, `Field Club`, `botanical walks`, `Grugeon`, `Shurlock`;
- local London newspapers;
- *Science-Gossip* and other natural-history serials;
- WMC reports/programmes;
- club rules / prospectus / minutes / circulars.

Record separately:

`proposal | organizing meeting | first excursion | officers elected | rules adopted | naming | first Journal report`

A multi-stage formation may dissolve the apparent date conflict.

## Stop rule

Use **1893 as the strongest current working date**, not as an unqualified final fact. Do not erase 1892 from the ledger. The purpose of this control is to preserve the evidence hierarchy while directing the next source closure.

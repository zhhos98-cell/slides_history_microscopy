# Working-men's education and microscope-slide network map — v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Branch:** `research/working-mens-microscopy-19c`

This file converts the national reconnaissance into **relations to test**. It privileges slide/preparation movement over institutional resemblance.

## 1. London: the most complete social-to-material chain

```text
Working Men's College (1854)
        |
        | teaching / former pupils
        v
M. C. Cooke
        |
        | 1862: former pupils + specimen interchange
        v
Society of Amateur Botanists
        |
        | organizational model
        v
Quekett Microscopical Club (1865)
        |
        +--> general preparation collection
        |       `-- >2,000 preparations by 1873
        |       `-- member borrowing
        |       `-- slide exchange
        |
        +--> object exhibition / practical instruction
        |
        `--> members / preparers / dealers / periodicals
```

**Closure still needed:** contributor-level crosswalk between WMC pupils, Society of Amateur Botanists members, early Quekett members and named slide donations/preparations.

This is where the branch can move beyond the historiographical claim that popular microscopy inherited WMC pedagogy. The hard test is whether the **same people who passed through the educational network can be seen preparing, donating, borrowing, exchanging or teaching with actual slides.**

## 5. Manchester: two educational systems occupy the same urban infrastructure

```text
Manchester Mechanics' Institution
        |
        +--> rooms used by Manchester Working Man's College (1858-61)
        |       `-- chemistry / physiology among subjects
        |
        `--> 1879 founding meeting
                v
        Manchester Microscopical Society
        low subscription / popular basis
```

The microscopical society should not automatically be labelled a successor to the WMC. The stronger claim presently available is that the **same mechanics-institute infrastructure repeatedly hosted attempts to broaden higher/scientific culture beyond elite societies.** Slide exchange and preparation instruction remain to be recovered.

This model warns against treating formal WMCs as the only relevant institution. In some industrial towns the carrier of microscopy was a **network of overlapping voluntary societies**, with working men moving between them.

## 11. Birmingham: institutional microscopy beside a commercial slide market

```text
Birmingham and Midland Institute
        |
        +--> artisan science lectures
        +--> Natural History Society
        `--> Microscopical Society

Birmingham commercial ecology
        |
        +--> J. Parkes & Son
        |       `-- microscopes + prepared slides / cheap sets (1857+)
        |
        `--> Southall Brothers & Barclay
                `-- prepared slides c.1890+
```

The missing edge is transaction evidence:

```text
local dealer/preparer  --[purchase / donation / member ownership]--> institute or society
```

That edge should be searched in accounts, advertisements, member papers, labels and surviving slides rather than inferred from geographic proximity.

## 13. Material relations to encode from now on

The branch should prioritize events that can be written in one of these forms:

```text
person --prepared--> slide
person --mounted--> slide
person --sold--> slide/set
institution --purchased--> slide/set
person --donated--> slide --> institution
institution --lent--> slide --> member
member --exchanged--> slide --> member
teacher --used in class--> slide
teacher --taught preparation of--> slide
society --exhibited--> slide/preparation
commercial dealer --supplied--> person/institution
working-men institution --generated--> microscopical society
```

Institutional similarity alone should not be promoted into this relation graph.

## 14. Current strongest object chains

1. **WMC/Cooke network → Quekett → >2,000 preparations + borrowing/exchange by 1873.**
2. **London Mechanics' Institute lineage → Birkbeck → Martin J. Cole → slide production + practical teaching + student supply.**
3. **A. B. Stirling → injected preparations → Thomas Craggs → Newcastle Mechanics' Institution soirée, 1867.**
4. **Bradford scientific/mechanics-institute ecology → surviving geological/botanical glass slides, provenance still to resolve.**

These five chains should anchor the next deep-source pass.

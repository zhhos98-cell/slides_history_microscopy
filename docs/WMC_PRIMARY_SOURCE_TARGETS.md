# Working-men's microscopy — slide-first primary-source targets

**Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Rule:** rank by expected yield of slide/preparation relations, not by institutional fame.

## Priority 1 — likely to produce named slides, makers, donors or exchanges

### Quekett Microscopical Club, 1865–1875

**Question:** can the London WMC / Society of Amateur Botanists genealogy be pushed down to named physical preparations?

Targets:
- early Council minutes and meeting minutes;
- donation lists and general-collection accession records;
- cabinet/catalogue lists;
- slide borrowing and exchange rules;
- names of contributors to the >2,000-preparation collection by 1873;
- named mounted objects exhibited at meetings;
- classes/instruction in mounting and preparation.

Crosswalk every actor against:
- Working Men's College pupils/teachers;
- Society of Amateur Botanists members;
- occupations and addresses;
- known professional/amateur mounters;
- surviving labelled slides.

**Desired closure:** `WMC pupil/member -> named Quekett slide/donation/exchange`.

## Source discipline

For each hit, record:

`date | institution | actor | occupation | slide/preparation | preparation method | maker | supplier | transaction | use context | later custody | exact source | confidence`

A source mentioning a microscope stays B unless a mounted/prepared object is identified. A source mentioning a specimen stays B unless mounting format is established. A commercial catalogue stays A_CONTEXT until a purchaser/use edge is closed.

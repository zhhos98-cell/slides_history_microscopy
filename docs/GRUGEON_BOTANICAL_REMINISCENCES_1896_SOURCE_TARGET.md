## 1. Source existence

John Ramsbottom's 1917 biographical notice on Alfred Grugeon explicitly says that most of his facts were derived from an **unpublished manuscript**:

> `Botanical Reminiscences`

Ramsbottom identifies it as a paper **read before the Lubbock Field Club in 1896**.

This is therefore not a hypothetical lost paper inferred from later biography. Its existence and use by Ramsbottom are directly attested in 1917.

Evidence state:

`EXISTENCE = CLOSED_1917_RETROSPECTIVE`

## 2. Why this source is unusually important

Ramsbottom's notice uses the manuscript in reconstructing Grugeon's:

- early self-directed botanical study;
- decision to attend M. C. Cooke's WMC botany lessons;
- examination/certification trajectory;
- teaching succession after Cooke;
- Society of Amateur Botanists membership/history;
- field collecting with Cooke;
- longer WMC botanical career.

If the manuscript survives, it could provide an actor-level retrospective connecting several otherwise separate source layers:

```text
pre-WMC botanical collecting
 -> Cooke's WMC teaching
 -> Grugeon's WMC teaching
 -> College magazine writing
 -> Society of Amateur Botanists
 -> field collecting / specimens
 -> later Lubbock Field Club
```

It should not be assumed that all these topics occur in the manuscript merely because Ramsbottom discusses them; the manuscript must be read before assigning individual statements to it.

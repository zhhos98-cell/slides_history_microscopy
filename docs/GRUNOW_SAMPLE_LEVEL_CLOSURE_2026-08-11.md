# Grunow sample-level closure — 2026-08-11

The NHM Wien 2023 publication and its supplementary transcription make it possible to move the Grunow case from an aggregate collection architecture to representative S-level sample chains.

## Sample 274

Grunow's own curation instruction gives No. 274, Jauling bei Berndorf, with a taxonomic pointer to *Navicula ambigua* forma *Craticula*. He states that one or two glass preparations and material in a bottle also exist for this number. He then explains how dried bottle material should be boiled with nitric acid, decanted with distilled water, spread onto coverslips, and remounted. Sample 274 therefore binds stored material, finished glass preparations, a catalogue address and a future remaking procedure.

## Sample 1363

A drawing annotation `1363. b (Cagliari) 12.9/32.9` for *Mastogloia Braunii* is explained as a direct microscope-stage address. The letter `b` distinguishes the second of several preparations of sample 1363; the two numerical coordinates tell the curator how to align the slide to recover the exact valve represented by the drawing. Drawing annotations also retained optical conditions such as magnification and prism/objective use.

## Sample 3073

For fossil Hungarian material, Grunow records multiple valves on preparation 3073b by stage coordinate. Examples include a *Navicula gemmata* fragment at `18/45.8`, *Actinoptychus Moronensis* at `13.8/42.1`, and a possible new *Navicula* at `8.3/44.3`. A circled coordinate means that the valve had already been drawn. The catalogue was therefore also a microscope map and a work-status register.

## Upgrade

The case is now S-level at representative historical sample level. The important point is that a Grunow sample number does more than identify a container. Depending on the sample, it can coordinate bottle material, glass preparations, slide variants, stage coordinates, drawings, optical conditions and maintenance instructions.

The current guard remains: no present-day JACQ/NHM persistent object record for 274, 1363 or 3073 was closed in this pass. Do not collapse the three examples into a claim that each Grunow sample retains every medium.

Primary sources: Naturhistorisches Museum Wien, Schuster et al. (2023), *Annalen des Naturhistorischen Museums in Wien*, B 125, 101–122; supplementary transcription of Grunow's eleven-page curation instructions.

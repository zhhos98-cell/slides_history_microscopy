# Grunow Vienna cross-media numbering upgrade — 2026-08-11

## Result

Upgrade Albert Grunow / NHM Vienna from A+ comparator to **S-level collection-system architecture**. Item-level closure of one historical sample number remains open.

NHM Vienna describes a complex Grunow diatom collection deposited at herbarium W in 1899. It includes unmounted material and capsules, fossil samples in historical pill boxes, historical and current microscope slides, a catalogue, ca. 15,000 drawings, and books annotated by Grunow. The curator's current description gives 3,278 sample numbers in the catalogue and ca. 1,400 surviving Grunow slides.

The important point is the catalogue's function. The 2023 NHM study states that it is organised by ascending sample number and can preserve species lists, locality, material origin, collector and collection number, exsiccata cross-references, preparation type, and even coordinates of individual valves on slides. Grunow also left eleven pages of instructions for managing the collection and catalogue.

## Mechanism

The sample number is therefore a cross-media address:

`raw/unmounted sample → catalogue → microscope slide/valve coordinate → drawing → annotated book → modern QR/JACQ identifier`.

This is stronger than simply saying that a collector numbered his samples. The number moves with the material into different observational and documentary states. It can allow a later taxonomist to recover type/original material by navigating between media.

The museum's present digitisation strategy actually confirms the same dependency. It begins with QR-coding and imaging the drawing collection because Grunow's drawings carry sample numbers and other details needed to build the taxonomic backbone for the rest of the collection; catalogue pages have also been imaged for transcription.

## Comparison

- **Cole:** one issue number binds paper, glass and cabinet sequence.
- **Cajal:** one preparation accumulates cabinet, inventory, archival and digital addresses.
- **Grunow:** one sample number crosses raw matter, glass, catalogue, drawing and annotated print.

These are three different address mechanisms, which is analytically cleaner than treating all historical slide numbers as one generic identifier type.

## Next bounded action

Choose one sample number explicitly discussed in the 2022/2023 NHM Grunow publications and close only that exemplar across catalogue entry, raw/capsule material, historical slide if extant, drawing, annotated book and current JACQ/QR address. One fully closed sample is more valuable than a partial global inventory.

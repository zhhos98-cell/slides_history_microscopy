01 Project

The object catalogue is one layer of a much larger microscopy backend.

The site now publishes the compact structure of the research corpus as well as the closed object survey: every core and extension bibliographic entry, BNA event/yield structures, and the project’s current analytical outputs. 08 Method

Evidence first; relations and storage layers remain typed.

The public site now mirrors the backend architecture: frozen object census, compact corpus metadata, research outputs, and large archival text masters remain distinct. The website can grow without turning presentation data into a new source of truth.

  1. 1
    Preserve identities.

    Document IDs, record IDs, occurrence locators, hashes and source URLs move with the compact export.

  2. 2
    Omit bulk text selectively.

    Page OCR and long raw previews stay in canonical masters when the site only needs navigational metadata.

  3. 3
    Publish structured findings.

    Extracted actors, events, prices, practices, clusters and crosswalks remain browseable.

  4. 4
    Keep claims reversible.

    Every derived relation points back to a source or a bounded research note with explicit limits.

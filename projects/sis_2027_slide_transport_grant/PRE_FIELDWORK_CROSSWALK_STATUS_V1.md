## 4. Bolton — exact operational archive closed, numbering crosswalk requires fieldwork

Archive-supplied finding aid directly closes:
- `FZ/14/1/1` — General and Committee Minutes, 1 Aug 1877–29 Oct 1880;
- `FZ/14/1/2` — General and Committee Minutes, 5 Nov 1880–8 Feb 1900;
- `FZ/14/2/1` — Account and Subscription Book, 1880–94;
- `FZ/14/3/1` — **Register of Slides Lent Out, May 1882–Jul 1887**;
- `FZ/14/3/2` — **Catalogue of Slides, Journals, and Books belonging to the Society, 1898**.

Direct printed primary evidence in *The Microscopical News and Northern Microscopist* (1881), p. 167 states that:
- the Society slide collection was being re-arranged after recent additions;
- Mrs Redmayne placed the late Redmayne's whole collection in the hands of the President and Secretary for selection;
- six dozen choice slides were selected;
- forty-eight choice diatom slides were also added;
- Society slides were lent to members.

This directly establishes the operation:
`private collection → officer custody/selection → Society collection → re-arrangement → member lending`.

### What cannot be done reliably before the visit

No public digital copy of `FZ/14/3/2` has been recovered in this pass, and public search does not expose its internal numbering/content.

Therefore do not invent or infer:
- catalogue numbers for the 72 Redmayne slides;
- catalogue numbers for the 48 diatom additions;
- identity between any 1882–87 lending-register number and the 1898 catalogue;
- return/damage/loss patterns before the register is inspected.

### Exact fieldwork crosswalk

1. Photograph `FZ/14/3/1` in full.
2. Identify numbering/subject/maker fields used in the lending register.
3. Search `FZ/14/3/2` for the same identifiers.
4. Use `FZ/14/1/1–2` to identify acquisition/re-arrangement decisions.
5. Use `FZ/14/2/1` only where a transaction/cabinet/replacement cost is suggested.

The strongest possible Bolton closure is:
`1880–81 accession/selection → 1882–87 named loan/return → 1898 catalogue persistence`.

Repair or damage is an optional upgrade, not a prerequisite.

# Redmayne object-recognition guard v1

Updated: 2026-09-13
Status: PRE-FIELDWORK IDENTIFICATION CONTROL

## 1. Why this note exists

The Bolton project hopes to identify at least some of the seventy-two slides selected from John Thomas Redmayne's private collection in 1880–81 and test whether they appear in the 1882–87 lending register and 1898 catalogue.

Current public photographs are useful for recognition, but they do **not** justify a simple visual maker-attribution rule.

## 2. What surviving photographed Redmayne slides show

Brian Stevenson's documented examples of Redmayne preparations show:
- diatom slides containing small selected groups of frustules;
- other slides containing strewn gatherings with many diatoms;
- labels written in **multiple handwriting styles** rather than one invariant visual template;
- a practice valued for clean/pure diatom preparations rather than one distinctive decorative mounting convention.

Contemporary/later commentary also indicates Redmayne mounted histological preparations, although such slides are now much less commonly encountered.

## 3. Attribution hierarchy for Bolton

Do **not** identify a slide as Redmayne merely from handwriting or visual resemblance.

Preferred order:

1. explicit maker/donor/origin field in `FZ/14/3/1` or `FZ/14/3/2`;
2. Redmayne grouping/series/provenance in 1880–81 minutes;
3. stable slide number carried from selection/loan/catalogue records;
4. subject/taxon consistency with known Redmayne material;
5. physical label/handwriting comparison only as corroboration.

A visual match without documentary provenance remains a candidate only.

## 4. Why this matters for the argument

The article is about institutional operations, not connoisseurial attribution.

The strongest Bolton result would be:
`documented Redmayne-origin preparation → Society address → named loan → return/repeat use → 1898 catalogue`.

It does not require identifying every surviving or recorded Redmayne slide.

## 5. Field capture if physical slides survive

For any candidate Redmayne preparation photograph:
- recto and verso full slide;
- label wording exactly;
- handwriting/printed label distinction;
- any maker initials/name/address;
- cabinet/catalogue/loan numbers;
- taxon/subject;
- cover shape and mount type;
- later labels or institutional marks;
- dimensions.

Keep historical maker attribution separate from later Society numbering and modern collection marks.

## 6. Claim ceiling

Safe:
`Surviving photographed Redmayne slides show heterogeneous label handwriting and mounting formats; documentary provenance should therefore lead attribution.`

Unsafe:
`All Redmayne slides can be recognised by a distinctive label style.`

## Trip 1 — Oxford HSM / RMS

### 4 research days / 4 nights

Travel model:
- arrive Oxford the evening before Day 1;
- four full reading-room/research days;
- return to London after Day 4.

This protects archive time and is preferable to saving one hotel night by sacrificing the beginning of Day 1 to a London–Oxford journey.

### Core records

Event-led rather than linear reading:
- Box 31 — ordinary/annual minutes, Jul 1868–Feb 1883;
- Box 38 — council minutes, Feb 1863–Jul 1880;
- Box 28 — council minutes, Oct 1880–Dec 1893;
- Box 41 — ledger, 1841–96;

- Box 103 — catalogue of microscopes/apparatus + Donations to Collection;
- Boxes 107–110 — selected cash-book periods;
- RMS MS 5, MS 13, MS 18 — bounded historical slide catalogues where needed.

### Day 1 — calibrate custody language

Start from published dated events:
- 12 Jan 1870 — T. H. Browne, 12 bone sections;
- early 1873 — Alfred U. Allen, coke-vapour slides;
- 9 Jun 1880 — Naples Station → A. W. Waters → RMS, 12 named slides;
- 11 May 1881 — Crisp, another Naples selection.

Question:
What did internal RMS records mean by `presented`, `received`, `donated` and `exhibited`?

Goal:
Distinguish permanent accession, temporary demonstration and other forms of custody before interpreting later events.

### Day 2 — Redmayne and selected 1880s accession events

High-priority Redmayne crosswalk:
- 1877 *Monthly Microscopical Journal* directly records *Micro-photographs from the Diatomaceae* as an author donation to the RMS Library and Cabinet;
- HSM currently preserves a matching Redmayne volume as **Royal Microscopical Society Collection [1978-665]**.

Test:
`1877 donation → internal RMS library/accession address → current HSM [1978-665]`.

Other dated hooks:
- 26 Apr 1882 — C. Baker / Naples preparations;
- 14 Mar 1883 — F. Jeffrey Bell / 19 Naples slides;
- 13 Jun 1883 — Charles Coppock / 5 donated slides;
- Mar 1884 — J. Norman jun. / 2 stickleback slides.

### Day 3 — register / financial / material operations

Use diagnostic dates rather than reading all accounts linearly.

Search for:

- glass/mounting/remounting;
- postage/carriage where relevant;

Box 49 and historical slide catalogues are used to establish older collection addresses before any modern HSM database crosswalk.

### Day 4 — current object/cabinet crosswalk

Use only diagnostic entries generated on Days 1–3.

Priorities:

## Trip 2 — Manchester + Bolton

### 2 research days / 2 nights

Travel model:
- travel London → Manchester the evening before Day 1;
- overnight Manchester;
- Day 1 Manchester Special Collections;
- second Manchester overnight;
- Day 2 Bolton History Centre;
- return Bolton/Manchester → London after the Bolton session.

Two nights protect both reading-room days. A one-night version would require a long-distance London–Manchester journey before Day 1 and would substantially reduce the already bounded Manchester research window.

### Day 1 — Manchester, bounded genealogy only

Archive:
`GB133-MIC` — Manchester Microscopical and Natural History Society.

Do not perform a broad institutional history.

Tests only:
- 1865 Oldham circulating-cabinet prehistory;
- 1866 `Mr T. Armstrong, Deansgate`;
- 1869 Manchester Circulating Microscopic Cabinet Society;
- whether later archive/retrospective material closes any predecessor continuity;
- whether `T. Armstrong` can be directly identified as Thomas Armstrong the Deansgate optical-instrument maker;
- bounded Redmayne/Tatham links only where catalogue records support them.

Stop if the archive begins effectively with the later Society and offers no predecessor bridge.

### Day 2 — Bolton, empirical centre

Archive:
`FZ/14` — Bolton Microscopical Society.

Exact priorities:
1. **`FZ/14/3/1` — Register of Slides Lent Out, May 1882–Jul 1887 — photograph in full first.**
2. `FZ/14/1/1–2` — minutes around 1880–82 Redmayne display/selection/re-arrangement and the May 1882 start of the lending register.
3. `FZ/14/2/1` — Account and Subscription Book, 1880–94, including membership lists; use for borrower/member crosswalk and diagnostic financial entries.
4. `FZ/14/3/2` — 1898 Catalogue of Slides, Journals, and Books; crosswalk diagnostic lending-register identifiers.

Historical sequence already controlled before travel:
- 19 Nov 1880 — third annual Conversazione displays Redmayne's microscopes, photomicrographs, pathological preparations and diatoms alongside instruments from major commercial makers;
- summer 1881 — Redmayne private collection placed with President/Secretary; 72 selected slides + 48 diatom slides added; Society cabinet being re-arranged; slides explicitly lent to members;
- May 1882 — dedicated lending register begins.

Main test:
`selection/re-arrangement → transaction-level member custody → return/repeat borrowing → 1898 catalogue persistence`.

Damage/repair is an optional upgrade, not a prerequisite.

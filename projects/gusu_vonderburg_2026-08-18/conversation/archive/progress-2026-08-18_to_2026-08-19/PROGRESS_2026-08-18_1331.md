### Why CMA-v1.5 = 113 is still provisional

`CMA-v1.5` uses bases `2025.19–2025.128`, counts ordinary objects once, counts covers 91/92 once while suppressing their dependent parts, and replaces cover 82 with its four components. This yields exactly 113 count units.

However, `2025.129` is independently verified as a March 2025 von der Burg purchase with the same acquisition date and J. H. Wade purchase context. No independent evidence has yet been found that excludes 129 from the museum's published phrase “113 Suzhou prints.” Therefore:

- `113` remains the museum's published acquisition count;
- `19–128 + record semantics = 113` is a useful working reconstruction;
- it is NOT yet a proven accession boundary;
- catalogue-level matching or another museum-side acquisition document is required to decide the status of 129.

### Current epistemic hierarchy

Use these labels going forward:

1. `verified_museum_record`: independently present in official museum API/site/bulk data.
2. `verified_record_semantics`: museum's own object/cover/component/part structure.
3. `verified_image_asset`: exact official image URL currently exposed.
4. `working_count_boundary`: boundary selected because it reproduces a published count; must not be promoted without external confirmation.
5. `book_concordance_verified`: requires catalogue-page/title/image evidence linking a book entry to museum record(s).

# Iron Library manuscript / estate census — total layer versus e-codices selection

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** ACTIVE COLLECTION-LEVEL CENSUS  
**Rule:** do not equate the 14 e-codices manuscripts with the total institutional manuscript/estate statistics, and do not infer the identities of `3 Nachlässe` from every donation called a `Nachlass`.

## 1. Current institution-level evidence

### Swiss ISIL

The current Swiss ISIL record for Eisenbibliothek (`CH-000728-7`) lists under `Archivbestände`:

- **66 Handschriften**;
- **3 Nachlässe**;
- c.300 graphic sheets;
- 80 maps of canton Schaffhausen.

### 2024 Annual Report — crucial current confirmation

The official 2024 annual-report statistics list under **Library / Form**:

- books/monographs;
- journals and periodicals;
- company publications and product catalogues;
- articles and offprints;
- **Separately listed collections and bequests**;
- digital resources.

Library scope is around **50,000 items**, 13th century to present.

Thus a current Library-side category of separately listed collections/bequests **still formally exists in 2024**. It is not safe to dismiss `Sondersammlungen/Nachlässe` as merely an obsolete legacy term.

At the same time, the same annual-report table separately reports:

- **Archive**: >130,000 records, >1,800 lm, c.2,900 3D objects;
- **Art collections**: c.1,000 works, explicitly including Schaffhausen historical maps/prints and metallurgy/technology-history art.

Therefore institution-level reporting and current catalogue signatures are nested rather than one-to-one.

## 4. Historical boundary evidence

### 4.1 Some former Eisenbibliothek `Sondersammlungen` are now EBA

A 2003 Eisenbibliothek collection description explicitly named as `Sondersammlungen`:

- the **Gonzen iron-mine archive with the scientific legacy of Willfried Epprecht**;
- the **Studiengesellschaft für die Nutzbarmachung schweizerischer Lagerstätten mineralischer Rohstoffe** archive.

Today these map to:

- **EBA 2 — Eisenbergwerk Gonzen**;
- **EBA 5 — Studiengesellschaft ...**.

ANTON transfer/provenance evidence is explicit:

- `EBA 2/1`: records/books/images from the **Nachlass Familie Neher** were incorporated into the GF Eisenbibliothek in 1950;
- `EBA 2/2`: Willfried Epprecht's testamentary bequest was made to Eisenbibliothek Paradies and is catalogued with provenance `Nachlass Willfried Epprecht`.

Thus `Eisenbibliothek Nachlass/Sondersammlung` in historical/institutional language does **not** guarantee present-day Library/IRONCAT placement.

### 4.2 Yet the category remains current in 2024

Annual reports from 2019 through **2024** list separately placed/listed collections and bequests under Library forms.

So the correct model is:

> **A current Library-side special-collection/bequest category exists, while historically similar collections have also been re-arranged into EBA/GFKS.**

This is a boundary-mapping problem, not a choice between “current” and “legacy”.

## 5. Estate-origin donations are not automatically the ISIL `3 Nachlässe`

Two explicit estate-origin donations are publicly visible:

- **Nachlass Henych** (2010), former GF foundry engineer; Czech-language foundry literature;
- **Nachlass Baldinger**: the 2019 annual report says all books from this estate had been catalogued; corresponding 2016 donation = Oskar Baldinger, industrial history / industrial archaeology.

However, the donations page states that ordinary donated books, **unlike Sondersammlungen**, are not kept closed/separate; they are integrated into systematic subject groups.

Therefore:

- `Henych` = **ESTATE-ORIGIN DONATION; CURRENT SEPARATELY-LISTED STATUS OPEN**;
- `Baldinger` = **ESTATE-ORIGIN DONATION; CURRENT SEPARATELY-LISTED STATUS OPEN**.

Neither should be counted as one of the ISIL `3 Nachlässe` without direct catalogue/staff confirmation.

## 7. Residency implications

### Scenario A — non-e-codices c.1850–1885 Library special collection exists

If the current separately listed layer contains a coherent nineteenth-century manuscript/estate corpus not publicly digitised, Library itself gains an additional strong onsite block.

### Scenario B — current Library special collections are mostly earlier/later or remotely substitutable

Then core remains Library printed/serial environment + EBA 3/EBA 4 + Rauschenbach.

### Scenario C — mixed current/legacy mapping

Some ISIL counts may map partly to current Library separately-listed holdings and partly to EBA/GFKS. This is now the key catalogue question and must be resolved by staff/export.

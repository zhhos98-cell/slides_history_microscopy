## 1. Core result

The institutional use of metallographic sections and photography predates the currently controlled external circulation system by several years.

The report:

**`Die Thätigkeit der Königlichen technischen Versuchsanstalten im Jahre 1894/95`**

published in *Stahl und Eisen*, 15 February 1896, summarises the annual report in the `Mittheilungen aus den Königlichen technischen Versuchsanstalten`.

Within the metal-testing department it explicitly records among the year's operations:

- **36 photographic recordings (`Photographische Aufnahmen`)**;
- preparation of **115 qcm of sections (`Schliffe`) for microscopic investigations**;
- colour investigations and annealing experiments in addition to the ordinary mechanical/technological tests.

## 2. Microscopy enters accident diagnosis and procurement/specification

The same report describes investigations of gas cylinders after an explosion on the exercise ground of the Royal Ballooning Department at Schöneberg.

The investigation included:

- extensive strength testing of the cylinder material;
- **microscopic examination of the material microstructure (`mikroskopische Untersuchungen des Materialgefüges`)**.

The report states that these investigations **led to the establishment of new conditions for the supply of such cylinders**.

This gives a controlled institutional chain by 1894/95:

**failure / explosion → material sampling → mechanical testing + microscopic microstructure examination → revised procurement/delivery conditions.**

Microscopy is therefore already connected to practical state material governance, not merely exploratory research.

## 3. Why this must be kept separate from external circulation

Two chronologies now need to be tracked independently.

### Internal production / operational use

By Rechnungsjahr **1894/95** the institute is already:

- preparing significant quantities of microscopic sections;
- making photographic recordings;
- using microstructure examination in accident/material diagnosis;
- allowing microscopic evidence to help reshape material-supply conditions.

### External circulation

The earliest currently controlled external circulation anchor remains Rechnungsjahr **1899**:

- Metallschliffe;
- corresponding microphotographs;
- prints from existing negatives for study purposes;
- supply against reimbursement of costs.

## 4. Analytical consequence

This creates a more precise institutional development:

**internal specimen/image production → diagnostic/testing use → procurement/specification effect → later external reproduction/circulation.**

It also strengthens the project's claim that microscopy was being integrated with other testing modalities before the turn-of-century image-distribution system became explicit.

By 1894/95 the microscope is already part of a compound evidential package alongside tensile tests, pressure tests, technological tests, photographic recording and official expert judgement.

## 5. Relation to the Wedding/Martens chain

The chronology is striking:

**1884 — Martens 120-section comparative collection**

→ **1885 — Wedding JISI microscopic-structure paper**

→ **1886 — institutional branch for preparation of sections for microscopic investigation**

→ **1894/95 — annual-report quantification: 115 qcm microscopic sections + 36 photographs; microscopy used in accident diagnosis / supply-condition revision**

→ **1895 — formal Metallographie department in the consolidated institutional structure**

→ **1899 onward — externally supplied sections / microphotographs / negative-based study prints.**

This is a strong institutionalisation path from preparation to operational testing to circulation.

## 6. Claim ceilings

Secure:

- the 1894/95 annual-report summary records 36 photographic recordings;
- it records 115 qcm of sections prepared for microscopic investigations;
- microscopic examination of gas-cylinder material was combined with extensive strength testing;
- the investigation led to new delivery/supply conditions for such cylinders.

Strong inference:

- metallographic evidence had become integrated into state/technical material diagnosis before the external teaching/reference circulation system is explicitly visible;
- internal specimen/image production and external circulation should be treated as distinct stages/variables.

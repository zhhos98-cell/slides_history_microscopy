# Exact-source request — Droste microphotographic glass plates

Prepared: 2026-09-11
Status: READY_FOR_STAFF_ENQUIRY; not yet sent

## Purpose

Resolve the three original early microphotographic glass plates discussed and illustrated by Bernhard Droste in *Ferrum* 93 (2024), p. 131, without expanding into a generic catalogue search.

## Controlled identifiers and publication routes

- Droste figure 2: glass plates with microphotographic images from the `königlichen mechanisch-technischen Versuchsanstalt zu Berlin`, shown with a publication plate.
- Droste `Bildnachweise`, item 2: `Foto: Eisenbibliothek, G 745,1`.
- Droste `Bildnachweise`, item 1: Martens, `Die mikroskopische Ausrüstung der königlichen mechanisch-technischen Versuchsanstalt` (1891), pp. 278–293, Tafeln IV–VI; `Foto: Eisenbibliothek, Per765`.
- Martens, `Ueber die Ausstellung der Versuchsanstalt auf der Weltausstellung zu Chicago im Jahr 1893` (1893), pp. 247f., Tafeln IV–VI.
- Martens, `Das mikroskopische Gefüge von Flusseisen in gegossenen Blöcken` (1893), pp. 273–292, Tafeln VII–XIII.
- Martens and Heyn, `Ueber die Mikrophotographie im auffallenden Licht und über die mikrophotographischen Einrichtungen der Königlichen Mechanisch-Technischen Versuchsanstalt in Charlottenburg` (1899), p. 73 onward.

## Questions for Iron Library staff

1. What does the identifier `G 745,1` denote in the Iron Library catalogue / image system: the three physical glass plates, a grouped object record, the photograph reproduced in *Ferrum*, or another record?
2. Are the three original glass plates individually catalogued? If so, what are their present signatures, accession numbers or object identifiers?
3. Is there catalogue or object-level documentation for their date, dimensions, photographic process, inscriptions/labels, image subjects, provenance and physical condition?
4. Can the printed plate shown beside the glass plates in Droste figure 2 be identified with a particular plate in one of Martens's 1891 or 1893 publications, especially the 1893 `Das mikroskopische Gefüge von Flusseisen in gegossenen Blöcken`, Tafeln VII–XIII?
5. What does `Per765` denote, and is it catalogically or materially related to `G 745,1`?
6. Does `A346`, also listed in Droste's image credits, belong to the same Martens/Heyn material-testing group or to a separate item?
7. Are digital images of the three original plates and their versos/labels available for research use?
8. If the plates are not represented in the public catalogue, may they be consulted onsite, and under which object/call number should a visit request be made?

## Evidence requested if available

Prefer item-level catalogue records, accession documentation, object photography (recto/verso), dimensions, inscriptions, container/envelope information, provenance notes and any publication cross-reference. A simple statement that `G 745,1` is an image reference rather than an object signature would itself close an important ambiguity.

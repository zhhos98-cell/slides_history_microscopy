## 5. Normalprofile — causal warning

Reject the smooth chain:

`Swiss metrication -> German Normalprofile -> merchant standardization`.

Literature control indicates that German Normalprofile were only weakly explained by metrication. Late-1870s overcapacity, engineering/railway user requirements, calculation needs and proprietary profile proliferation mattered. The Normalprofile commission itself sought sales/demand statistics from German rolling works.

Use instead:

`PRODUCER PROFILE VARIETY -> MARKET / SALES OBSERVATION -> TECHNICAL SELECTION -> NORMAL SERIES -> PRODUCER / MERCHANT / USER UPTAKE`.

Metric notation is an empirical field, not the master cause.

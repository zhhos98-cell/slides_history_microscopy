## 4. Secure 1885 publication hinge

A contemporary *Schweizerische Bauzeitung* notice, Bd. V, Nr. 12 (1885), titled `Profil-Album von Schinz & Bär in Zürich und Basel`, says the iron merchant had **recently issued** the album.

It describes:

- a broad building-profile repertoire;
- Winkel- and T-Eisen;
- Treppenbeschlag- and Fenstereisen;
- a rich range of Gesimseisen;
- the latter speciality groups as **newly created and protected by Schinz & Bär**, absent from other price books;
- Gesimseisen as enabling a more decorative treatment of iron construction.

It also reports a review in **The Ironmonger, 28 February 1885** praising the execution of the drawings and the publication. The original *Ironmonger* issue has **not yet been independently inspected**.

Source:
- https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=sbz-002%3A1885%3A5%3A%3A125

## 5. New external carrier ecology, 1872–1890

The 1885 album now sits inside a denser independently documented sequence.

### 5.1 1872–74 — external transaction account

Wirtschaftsarchiv Vorarlberg `AT-WAV-01-041`, Josef Pircher Hauptbuch Nr. 13 (1872–74), includes an account entry/index for:

- `Schinz & Bär` — **`Eisenhalle in Zürich`** — account/page 177.

Source:
- https://wirtschaftsarchiv-v.at/images/Pircher/01-041-Pircher_Hauptbuch13.pdf

This proves the `Eisenhalle` identity was already legible in a transregional commercial bookkeeping network. The underlying account contents remain to be recovered before making product-level claims.

### 5.2 1887 — office, magazine and main depot

Stadtarchiv Zürich `V.L.226.:259`, Schinz & Baer letterhead, 20 Aug 1887:

- `Büro und Magazin Eisenhalle`;
- `Hauptdepot Lagergasse`.

Source:
- https://amsquery.stadt-zuerich.ch/detail.aspx?ID=208893

This ties business correspondence directly to a physical stock / logistics topology.

### 5.3 1889 — technical-press ordering identity

A *Schweizerische Bauzeitung* advertisement, 25 May 1889, gives:

- `SCHINZ & BAER`;
- Zürich / Waaggasse / `z. Eisenhalle`;
- telegram address **Eisenhalle**;
- telephone no. 969;
- `Eiserne Tragbalken & Säulen` and `alle Bauartikel in Eisen und Guss`.

Source:
- https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=sbz-002%3A1889%3A13%3A%3A220

The same name therefore functioned as physical stock location, telegraphic address, advertisement identity and order interface.

### 5.4 1889 — surviving illustrated `Bauartikel-Lager` folding prospectus

Staatsarchiv Thurgau:

- `Slg. 15, 8'4/105` — `Schinz und Bär, Eisenhandlung, Zürich und Basel: Faltprospekt zu ihrem Bauartikel-Lager mit Illustrationen ...`, dated 1889.

Catalogue context:
- https://query-staatsarchiv.tg.ch/archivplansuche.aspx?ID=344444

This is now a **HIGH-PRIORITY external morphology control** between the 1885 and 1896 public albums. Do not infer that it is derived from the 1885 album until profile/page concordance proves it.

### 5.5 1889/90 — technical-school album circulation already occurred

A Winterthur Technikum annual-report donations/acquisitions list, signed at Winterthur **15 March 1890**, records:

- donor: `Von den Herren Schinz & Bär in Zürich`;
- object: **`1 Partie Albums von Profil-Eisen.`**

Source:
- https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=twj-002%3A1889%3A16%3A%3A56

This is direct evidence that Schinz & Bär supplied **multiple / a batch of profile-iron albums to a technical educational institution by 1889/90**.

This corrects the earlier shorthand that educational circulation only becomes explicit in 1896. The 1896 publication statement should now be read as later confirmation / expansion of an already demonstrable practice, not necessarily its beginning.

Do not yet assert that the Technikum batch consisted of the 1885 edition, that students actually used the albums in class, or that the quantity is known.

## 6. 1896 public carrier — quantified / standard-integrated reference

The contemporary 1896 *Bauzeitung* review describes Bär & Co's `Façoneisen-Album` as combining:

- commonly used **Normalprofile**;
- U / T / angle / Zorès / Quadrant and related profiles;
- **weight, dimension and moment-of-inertia tables**;
- load-bearing data up to 12 m;
- Bär proprietary profiles for cornice, showcase and railing constructions.

The firm offered the album to interested users and to Polytechnikum / Technikum in desired quantities so students could become accustomed to Normalprofile.

Source:
- https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=sbz-002%3A1896%3A28%3A%3A214

A direct Zürich merchant counterpart's archive preserves this 1896 album/catalogue with the *Bauzeitung* extract.

### Longitudinal comparison

**1885 public regime:**
`profile repertoire + proprietary/protected specialities + drawing quality + building/design applications`

**1889/90 circulation regime:**
`illustrated building-stock prospectus + technical-school batch album distribution`

**1896 public regime:**
`Normalprofile + dimensional/weight/inertia/load data + proprietary profiles + explicit technical-school distribution`.

The historical question is what was added, retained, reordered, quantified, spatially tied to stock or redistributed between these regimes.

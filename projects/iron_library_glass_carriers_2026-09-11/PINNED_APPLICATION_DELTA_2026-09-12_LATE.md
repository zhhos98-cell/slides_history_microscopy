## 1. Do not use the old equal-cluster shorthand uncritically

Old shorthand:

`Haffter c.1858–67 -> bridge 1868–72 -> Küderli 1877–85`

is now too symmetrical.

### Corrected geometry

- **Haffter:** proven large and unusually coherent early-19th-century trade/calculation/correspondence infrastructure; several relevant series extend to 1867, but late-1850s/1860s page/entry density is **OPEN** and must be physically validated before treating 1858–67 as an equally massive core.
- **Küderli/Bär:** currently the **cleanest compact application core**, because the archive itself preserves a chronologically diagnostic sequence across changing supply geography and product-document regimes.

## 3. Current question — sharpened

Do **not** pitch generic `standardization`.

Use:

> **How did iron merchants make heterogeneous rolled-iron products commercially comparable across changing suppliers, routes, maker-specific profile series, dimensions, weights, prices and orders — and what changed when association-level Normalprofile entered that already-existing comparison system?**

The novelty target is the **merchant documentary mediation layer** between producer-specific product systems and engineering/user standards.

## 4. Causal warning

Reject the smooth chain:

`Swiss metrication -> German Normalprofile -> merchant standardization`.

Literature control indicates the relationship between metrication and German Normalprofile was weak; late-1870s market overcapacity, engineering/railway user requirements, calculation needs and producer-specific profile proliferation were important drivers.

Treat units/metric notation as an empirical field, not as the master cause.

## 5. Four live hypotheses for EBA 4/14

1. **Supplier substitution:** album changes mainly reflect France -> Germany supplier geography.
2. **Merchant commensurability:** album is an internally constructed equivalence/comparison system across heterogeneous products.
3. **Normalprofil uptake:** a documentary break appears c.1879–81 in terminology, numbering, dimensions or organization.
4. **Mixed coexistence:** proprietary and formal normal series coexist and still require merchant equivalence work.

No hypothesis is admitted until physical inspection.

# Schinz & Bär pre-album public stock-reference layer, 1876

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** SECURE PRE-`EBA 4/14` PUBLIC CONTROL / NEW EARLY HINGE  
**Purpose:** establish what Schinz & Bär publicly made legible about iron stock immediately before the Iron Library `EBA 4/14 Profileisenalbum` series begins in 1877.

## 0. Current conclusion

Advertisements in *Die Eisenbahn* show that in **1876**, immediately before `EBA 4/14` begins, Schinz & Bär's Zürich `Eisenhalle` was already publicly representing rolled iron stock through **profile / dimensional classes**.

A repeated advertisement for rolled iron girders states that they were:

> `vorräthig in Profilen von 80 bis 400 Millimeter Höhe`.

The same advertisement places these alongside a wider stocked repertoire including:

- `Façon- und Constructions-Eisen`;
- gas pipes;
- leaded sheet iron;
- tinplate;
- zinc sheet;
- roof windows (`Dachfenster`);
- door panels;
- wheel guards and other building-use iron articles.

Public controls:
- *Die Eisenbahn*, 1 Dec. 1876, advertisement.
- *Die Eisenbahn*, 22 Dec. 1876, repeated advertisement.
- E-Periodica digitisation.

The secure transition is therefore no longer forced to begin with the 1881 proprietary roof-profile article.

We now have:

**`1876 PUBLIC STOCK DESCRIPTION BY PROFILE / HEIGHT RANGE`**
→ **`EBA 4/14 PROFILEISENALBUM STARTS 1877`**
→ `1881 illustrated protected Schinz & Bär roof-profile system`
→ `1885 public Schinz & Bär Profil-Album`
→ `1896 new/current edition with profile-local quantitative tables`.

---

## 1. What the 1876 advertisement securely shows

### A. `Profil` already belongs to the public sales language

The rolled beams are not advertised only as undifferentiated tonnage or generic `Tragbalken`; the advertisement tells users that stock exists in **profiles spanning a dimensional range of 80–400 mm height**.

This is evidence for a public stock-description regime in which cross-sectional product form and a salient dimension already structured availability.

### B. The public statement is coarse-grained

The advertisement, as currently recovered, does **not** publicly expose:

- a numbered profile-by-profile list;
- individual drawn cross-sections;
- per-profile weight;
- moment of inertia;
- load table;
- proprietary identity for each beam profile.

Do not infer that such information did not exist elsewhere in the firm. The claim is only that **this public advertisement** presents availability at a coarser dimensional/category level.

### C. The catalogue/archive question becomes sharper

`EBA 4/14` starts the following year. Its first onsite function test should now ask:

> **Does the album unpack the coarse public category `Profile 80–400 mm Höhe` into a more granular reference system — individual shapes, dimensions, suppliers, numbers, prices or pasted producer sheets?**

This is answerable from the physical album and cannot be recovered from the advertisement alone.

---

## 2. Earlier 1876 Eisenhalle evidence

A March 1876 *Die Eisenbahn* advertisement from `SCHINZ & BAER, z. Eisenhalle, Zürich` separately advertises drawn wrought-iron patent gas pipes from 1/4 to 3 English inches internal diameter plus fittings, cast-iron pipes, hydrants and valves.

This matters because it shows the same firm already using **dimensional range + component/fitting vocabulary** to make stock/orderability public before the profile albums.

It should not be conflated with profile-iron numbering. It is a parallel example of dimensional stock description.

## 3. Revised reference-system chronology

### Layer 0 — public stock / dimensional availability, 1876

`PRODUCT FAMILY + DIMENSIONAL RANGE + STOCK CLAIM`.

### Layer 1 — archival album begins, 1877

`EBA 4/14`, physical/reference function OPEN.

### Layer 2 — proprietary illustrated cross-section, 1881

Schinz & Bär `Neue Bedachungsprofile`: an engineered, illustrated, firm-specific system said to be patented in several countries.

### Layer 3 — public album, 1885

A rich public repertoire including ordinary profiles and protected Schinz & Bär specialities.

### Layer 4 — revised quantified edition, 1896

Normalprofile + proprietary forms + dimensions/weights/moments of inertia/load information, with quantitative table data now placed at the relevant profiles.

### Layer 5 — distributed/updateable reference copies, 1903–1907

New album batches and supplement sheets to technical education.

This is not a claim of smooth cumulative progress. The onsite task is to identify which information operations are actually materialised in `/14` and `/13`.

## 4. New central comparison: granularity of identity

The strongest question generated by the 1876 evidence is:

> **At what level was a product made individually identifiable?**

Possible levels:

1. generic product family — `Tragbalken`;
2. dimensional range — `80–400 mm Höhe`;
3. named profile family;
4. drawn individual cross-section;
5. numbered profile;
6. profile + dimensions;
7. profile + price/order key;
8. profile + engineering quantities;
9. proprietary / Normalprofil status;
10. versioned identity across editions/supplements.

The archive can test movement between these levels without presuming that later granularity simply replaced earlier regimes.

## 6. Claim ceiling

Secure:

- 1876 Schinz & Bär / Eisenhalle advertisements publicly offer rolled iron girders in profiles spanning **80–400 mm height**;
- the same advertisement situates these within a broader building-iron stock repertoire;
- `EBA 4/14` begins in 1877, one year after this controlled public stock-description evidence.

Strong research inference:

- the 1876 advertisement supplies a pre-album baseline for **reference granularity**;
- comparing it with `/14`, 1881, 1885 and 1896 allows a materially specific study of how profile identity became more granular and differently formatted.

Do not assert:

- that 1876 advertisements exhaust the firm's internal information system;
- that profile numbers were absent internally;
- that `/14` was created because of the 1876 sales regime;
- that `/14` begins with exactly the same 80–400 mm beam family;
- that the later proprietary/Normalprofil system is a linear evolutionary outcome of the 1876 advertisement.

## Resume point

Use **1876 public dimensional stock description** as the earliest tight morphology/reference baseline immediately preceding the five-volume EBA 4 profile-album sequence.

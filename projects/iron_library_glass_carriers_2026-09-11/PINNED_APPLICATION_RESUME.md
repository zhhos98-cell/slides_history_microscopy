## Current chronological result

The holdings are **overlapping temporal islands, not one uniformly thick archive**.

- **LIBRARY:** the only system that remains broadly dense across the entire nineteenth century; subject shelves + serial runs are the continuous backbone.
- **EBA 3 Haffter:** exceptionally dense c.1835–1867, then tails off. Do not treat its 1722–1983 envelope as uniform.
- **EBA 4 Küderli/Bär:** compact but unusually clean c.1854–1885 bridge corpus; correspondence 1858–72, price lists 1878–80, profile albums 1877–85, cashbook from 1884, sales journal from 1885.
- **EBA 6 Swiss iron trade:** 39 lm and nominally 1852–2011, but the coherent association/convention layer currently becomes visible mainly around 1889/90 and later. **Do not use the 1852 start date as evidence of a thick mid-century corpus without further proof.**
- **native GF material in GFA 1 before 1885:** richer than isolated singletons — a real product/prospectus/price-list/works-document cluster exists — but it still behaves as surviving islands inside a retrospective historical archive, not a regular annual corporate registry.
- **Rauschenbach:** currently the strongest genuinely continuous pre-1890 industrial-company block in the GFA environment. Its pre-1885 strength lies in operational/company records (correspondence/contracts, staff/social records, property, technical classes), while formal AG governance begins only in **1892** despite the GFA 51 envelope beginning in 1865.
- **Traisen:** newly visible 1870s–80s industrial-technical node. The four-volume Blaubuch sequence carries 1872–92 content but the catalogue explicitly documents later copies/copying activity; by contrast, GFA 1/106 explicitly includes **original 1880 tempering-furnace plans**. Keep content chronology and surviving-carrier chronology separate.
- **Personnel registers:** some later-created registers contain entries reaching back to 1855/1872; classify these as `LATER_REGISTER_WITH_EARLIER_DATA`, not automatically as contemporary 1850s/70s records.
- **1896:** clean GF corporate caesura. GFA 2 Verwaltungsrat/Konzernleitung begins with the joint-stock-company era and marks the sharp thickening of systematic corporate governance records.

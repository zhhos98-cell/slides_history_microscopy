# Current holdings architecture — institutional reporting versus catalogue implementation

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** ACTIVE CURRENT-ARCHITECTURE MAP  
**Rule:** the institution has more than one valid organizational layer. Do not force the 2024 annual-report categories and the ANTON signature systems into a one-to-one correspondence.

## 1. Two current architectures coexist

### 1.1 Institution/reporting architecture — 2024 Annual Report

The official **2024 Annual Report** gives three top-level holdings/reporting columns:

1. **Library** — around **50,000 items**, 13th century to present;
2. **Archive** — more than **130,000 records**, more than **1,800 linear meters**, approx. **2,900 three-dimensional objects**, 16th century to present;
3. **Art collections** — approx. **1,000 works**, 15th century to present.

The Library `Form` column still explicitly includes:

> **Separately listed collections and bequests**

alongside books/monographs, journals/periodicals, company publications/product catalogues, articles/offprints and digital resources.

The Art collections column explicitly includes historical Schaffhausen maps/prints and works on metallurgy/history of technology.

### 1.2 Catalogue implementation — current ANTON root

Current ANTON exposes four signature systems:

| Signature | Current title | Date envelope | Physical extent | Governing collection logic |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **GFA** | Georg Fischer Archiv | 1060–2026 | c. **1070 lm** | corporate/family/subsidiary/administrative/media/object provenance, including retrospective historical holdings |
| **EBA** | Eisenbibliothek Archiv | 1438–2025 | c. **90 lm** | non-GF archival fonds and special collections administered within the Iron Library/archive environment |
| **GFKS** | Georg Fischer Kunstsammlung | 1525–2025 | c. **48 lm** | physical art, maps, prints, views, sculpture/objects |
| **GFD** | Georg Fischer Dokumentation | 0050–2025 | c. **1 lm** | modern digital documentation/reference-image system |

The research architecture is therefore:

> **IRONCAT / Library** + **ANTON: EBA / GFA / GFKS / GFD**, nested inside a broader reporting model of **Library / Archive / Art collections**.

Do not assume one-to-one equivalence between reporting columns and signature systems.

## 2. Why the distinction matters

There are at least three independent questions:

1. who owns/administers the material institutionally?
2. where is it reported statistically?
3. where is it catalogued/retrieved today?

A historical Eisenbibliothek `Sondersammlung` can now sit in EBA. Historical Schaffhausen maps can now appear in GFKS while ISIL retains older institution-level map figures.

## 4. Art collections / GFKS — current composition is now quantifiable

The 2024 annual report gives a remarkably precise anatomy of the institution's art collections.

### 4.1 Four managed collection components

1. **GF's organically grown art collection** — approx. **430 works**, 17th–21st century, including paintings, watercolours, prints and 3D objects.
2. **A Schaffhausen private map collection** acquired c.30 years ago and preserved as an integral unit — **98 historical maps of the Canton of Schaffhausen**.
3. **A second Schaffhausen private print collection**, also preserved as an integral unit — approx. **366 prints** (woodcuts, engravings, steel engravings, aquatints, lithographs, etc.) of the Schaffhausen region, 16th–19th century.
4. **Iron Library graphic-art collection** — **109 works** (prints, maps, sketches, photographs, drawings, certificates) on metallurgy and the history of technology.

These four figures total approx. **1003 works**, effectively explaining the annual-report overview figure of `c.1000 works`.

This also strongly suggests that the Swiss ISIL figures `80 Schaffhausen maps / c.300 graphical sheets` are older or differently scoped counts related to present art/special-collection holdings rather than separate additive blocks. This is an inference, not yet an item-level identity claim.

### 4.2 Current catalogue relation

The annual report states that the art collections are catalogued/described in detail in the **Corporate Archives database**, consistent with their present visibility through ANTON / GFKS and related special-collection records.

Current ANTON examples include:

- 1866 `Album von Schaffhausen` group;
- `Schaffhausen, Stahlfabrik`, 1866;
- Schaffhausen maps 1879–85 and 1883.

### 4.3 Digitisation status — important correction

Do not say GFKS is already fully digitised.

In 2024 a **multi-year professional digitisation project** began/continued:

- first tranche: approx. **400 objects** professionally photographed in 2024;
- focus included large-format works, 3D objects and lithographs in plan drawers;
- project explicitly scheduled to continue in 2025;
- displayed works at Paradies can also be browsed remotely through Smartify.

Current ANTON already exposes many `Digitalisiert` records, but 2024 evidence shows full professional digitisation was still incomplete at that point.

### 4.4 c.1850–1885 structural rating

GFKS / managed art layer contains a real nineteenth-century Schaffhausen visual corpus, but its logic differs from operational archives:

- scale: HIGH;
- seriality: MEDIUM overall, HIGH inside some integral private collections / albums;
- c.1850–85 density: MEDIUM;
- provenance coherence: mixed — some collections deliberately preserved as integral units, larger GF collection accumulated organically;
- originality: HIGH;
- remote substitutability: MEDIUM–HIGH and increasing with digitisation;
- onsite value: materiality/verso/framing/condition/cross-object comparison, not necessarily basic image access.

Current tier: **Tier B supporting visual/original-object system**.

## 6. Special-collection boundary history — controlled at several dates

### 6.1 2003: explicit Eisenbibliothek `Sondersammlungen`

A 2003 Iron Library description called the following `Sondersammlungen`:

1. Gonzen iron-mine archive + Willfried Epprecht scientific legacy;
2. Studiengesellschaft für die Nutzbarmachung schweizerischer Lagerstätten mineralischer Rohstoffe.

Today they map to **EBA 2** and **EBA 5**.

`EBA 2/1` and `EBA 2/2` explicitly document Neher/Epprecht provenance transfers/bequests to Eisenbibliothek.

### 6.2 2019–2024: Library still officially has separately listed collections/bequests

Annual reports through 2024 continue to place separately listed collections/bequests under Library.

Therefore:

> a current Library-side special-collection/bequest category exists, while historically similar collections have also been rearranged into present EBA/GFKS systems.

### 6.3 Swiss ISIL figures

Current ISIL `CH-000728-7` lists:

- 66 manuscripts;
- 3 estates;
- c.300 graphical sheets;
- 80 Schaffhausen maps.

The 2024 art-collection anatomy now makes it especially unsafe to add `300/80` as independent holdings outside current art/EBA systems.

### 6.4 Estate-origin donations are not automatically the ISIL three

Henych and Baldinger are publicly visible estate-origin donations, but ordinary donations are integrated by subject rather than necessarily preserved as closed Sondersammlungen.

Neither is identified as one of the ISIL `3 estates` without direct confirmation.

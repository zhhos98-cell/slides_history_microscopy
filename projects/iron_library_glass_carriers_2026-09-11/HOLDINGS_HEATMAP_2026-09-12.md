**Date:** 2026-09-12

**Rule:** collection first; question second; onsite necessity third. Rank collection blocks by archival structure before selecting a research problem.

## 0. Current retrieval architecture

Use current retrieval boundaries:

1. **LIBRARY / IRONCAT** — classified printed holdings, serials, rara, plus a separately-listed manuscript/special-collection layer whose current internal mapping is partly OPEN.
2. **EBA / ANTON** — Eisenbibliothek Archiv, non-GF archival fonds and special collections.
3. **GFA / ANTON** — Georg Fischer Archiv, family/company/subsidiary/administrative/media/object provenance.
4. **GFKS / ANTON** — Georg Fischer Kunstsammlung, physical art/maps/prints/views/objects.
5. **GFD / ANTON** — Georg Fischer Dokumentation, modern digital documentation/reference-image layer.

The institution's 2024 reporting model (`Library / Archive / Art collections`) is a broader management view; do not force a one-to-one correspondence with current ANTON systems. Historical collection-management boundaries also differ from today's catalogue boundaries.

## 1. Six axes

Assess every block independently on:

1. **Scale** — physical extent / volumes / description units / title count.
2. **Seriality** — whether recurring internally ordered series survive.
3. **Date density** — actual c.1850–1885 concentration, not envelope dates.
4. **Provenance coherence** — whether the block accrued through one identifiable activity/institution.
5. **Contemporaneity / originality** — contemporary original vs later copy/register/dossier.
6. **Onsite value / remote substitutability** — physical evidence not replaceable by public facsimiles, alternative library access, staff support or reference images.

Ratings: `VERY HIGH / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / VERY LOW / OPEN`.

Carrier vocabulary:

- `ORIGINAL_CONTEMPORARY`
- `CONTEMPORARY_BUT_RETROSPECTIVELY_FILED`
- `LATER_COPY_OF_EARLIER_RECORD`
- `LATER_REGISTER_WITH_EARLIER_DATA`
- `RETROSPECTIVE_HISTORICAL_DOSSIER`
- `ORIGINALITY_OPEN`

Access rule:

> **RARITY != REMOTE NON-SUBSTITUTABILITY != ONSITE NECESSITY.**

## 3. Chronological cross-section

### c.1850–1867

- **Library:** continuous printed/serial backbone, structurally very high but access value mixed.
- **Haffter:** exceptionally dense contemporary trade/accounting/correspondence archive.
- **Küderli:** begins 1854.
- **Native GF:** product-document regime begins to become visible, not a regular corporate registry.
- **Rauschenbach:** technical/company presence exists, but public metadata does not prove a large continuous operational series.
- **GFKS:** clean 1866 Schaffhausen visual album node; strongly digitised.

### c.1868–1872 — first collection-driven overlap peak

- Library print/serials remain continuous.
- Haffter tail remains active.
- Küderli correspondence is active.
- Native-GF product/prospectus/price communication becomes clear.
- Rauschenbach contributes a company/labour node; `/806` begins 1871 and `/809` 1872, but broader dossier scale remains unresolved.
- GFKS supplies visual context, not the archival core.

### c.1877–1885 — second collection-driven overlap peak

- Library print/serials remain continuous.
- Küderli shifts to profile albums, price lists, cashbook and sales-journal onset.
- Native GF product/exhibition regime continues.
- Rauschenbach remains a potentially useful company comparator, **not yet a proven large operational archive**.
- Traisen contributes copied Blaubuch content plus original 1880 technical plans.
- EBA 1 contributes a late-19c engineering-map cluster with broad dates.
- GFKS supplies digitised maps/views/prints, useful chiefly as a separate visual evidence regime.

### 1889–1896 transition

- EBA 6 association/convention infrastructure becomes visible around 1889/90.
- Rauschenbach formal AG governance begins 1892.
- Native-GF systematic board/general-meeting/annual-report regimes begin 1896.

### Rauschenbach

Publicly proven serial core:

- `GFA 1/806` employee register 1871–1930 — 1 bound volume;
- `/809` sickness fund 1872–1930 — 10 bound volumes.

Broad one-file dossiers include `/780` 1868–1905, `/781` 1869–1921, `/786` 1878–1934, `/779` 1855–1921, `/782` 1800–1960 and `/2330` 1852–1981. `/2288` explicitly combines 1876–79 correspondence with 1928–41 agreements.

### GFKS

- 2024 institutional reporting: one integral private collection = **98 historical Schaffhausen maps**; another = **c.366 Schaffhausen prints from the 16th–19th century**.
- 1866 `Album von Schaffhausen` yields a coherent 24-local-view sequence, including Stahlfabrik and city views; public catalogue exposes many/all visible album entries as digitised.
- controlled maps within the window include `GFKS 2/861` Schaffhausen 1879–85 and `/862` canton map 1883; these prove presence, not a dense period-wide map run.

### Library access

- current e-rara Iron Library thematic collection: >140 titles / >44,000 pages;
- interlibrary consultation is available for Library literature in Switzerland and abroad;
- therefore printed subject depth is not equivalent to onsite necessity.

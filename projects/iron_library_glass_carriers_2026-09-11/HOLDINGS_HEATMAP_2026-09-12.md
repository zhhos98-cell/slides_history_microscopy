# Holdings heat map — nineteenth-century collection structure

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** ACTIVE STRUCTURAL MAP  
**Rule:** collection first; question second. Rank collection blocks by archival structure before selecting a research problem.

## Axes

Each block is assessed qualitatively on five independent axes:

1. **Scale** — physical extent / volumes / description units / title count.
2. **Seriality** — whether recurring internally ordered series survive.
3. **Date density** — how much material actually falls inside c.1850–1885 rather than merely sharing an envelope date.
4. **Provenance coherence** — whether the block accrued through one identifiable activity/institution rather than retrospective assembly.
5. **Contemporaneity / originality** — whether surviving carriers are contemporary originals rather than later copies, retrospective dossiers, or later registers containing earlier data.

Ratings: `VERY HIGH / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / OPEN`.

## Chronological cross-section

### c.1850–1867

- Library = continuous VERY HIGH backbone.
- EBA 3 Haffter = VERY HIGH contemporary correspondence/accounting/trade series.
- EBA 4 begins in 1854.
- Native GF = outgoing book, 1856 furnace plan, and emerging product documentation, but not a regular corporate registry.
- Rauschenbach = technical/industrial activity visible; exact item-level contemporaneous density still needs quantification.

### c.1868–1876

- Library remains VERY HIGH.
- Haffter tails off.
- EBA 4 correspondence remains active.
- Native GF product/prospectus/price-list cluster becomes much clearer.
- Rauschenbach gains correspondence/contracts and staff/social series.
- Traisen Blaubuch content begins 1872, but surviving GFA volumes are documented as copies / tied to copying activity.

### c.1877–1885

- Library remains VERY HIGH.
- EBA 4 is the cleanest non-GF archive fit: profile albums, price lists, cashbook/sales-journal onset.
- Native GF product/exhibition documentation continues.
- Rauschenbach is the strongest operational-company archive.
- Traisen adds original 1880 tempering-furnace plans alongside copied continuous Blaubuch content.
- Formal GF corporate governance still absent; Rauschenbach AG governance also does not begin until 1892.

### 1889–1896 transition

- EBA 6 association/convention infrastructure becomes visible around 1889/90.
- Rauschenbach formal AG governance begins in 1892.
- GF corporate governance becomes structurally thick with GFA 2 from 1896.

## Methodological rule forced by the map

Never equate the **historical date represented in a record** with the **creation date of the surviving carrier**.

Every final source list should label one of:

- `ORIGINAL_CONTEMPORARY`
- `CONTEMPORARY_BUT_RETROSPECTIVELY_FILED`
- `LATER_COPY_OF_EARLIER_RECORD`
- `LATER_REGISTER_WITH_EARLIER_DATA`
- `RETROSPECTIVE_HISTORICAL_DOSSIER`
- `ORIGINALITY_OPEN`

This distinction is now as important as fonds provenance and date range.

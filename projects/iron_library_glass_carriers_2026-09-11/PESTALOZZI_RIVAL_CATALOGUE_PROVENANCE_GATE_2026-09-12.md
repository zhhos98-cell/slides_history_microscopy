# Pestalozzi/Wiser rival-catalogue retention — provenance gate

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** SOURCE-CRITICAL CORRECTION / NEGATIVE CONTROL  
**Purpose:** prevent the archival adjacency of the 1896 Bär catalogue and 1899 Julius Schoch catalogue in Stadtarchiv Zürich `VII.382` from being mistaken for an original historical competitor-reference packet.

## 0. Governing correction

Stadtarchiv Zürich finding aid `VII.382 Pestalozzi + Co AG` lists:

- `4.4.7` — **Album (= Katalog) der Firma Baer & Co mit Auszug aus der Bauzeitung [Ueberformat]**, 1896;
- `4.4.8` — **Katalog der Firma Julius Schoch & Co, Schwarzhorn, Zürich von 1900 [Ueberformat]**, dated 1899 in the finding aid.

These two rival catalogues are therefore retained consecutively in the modern finding aid.

However, the parent class `4.4` is explicitly titled:

> **`Verschiedenes`**.

The same class contains heterogeneous materials such as:

- an undated goods-turnover item;
- a business card;
- notes on stock, partners and Wiser genealogy;
- an 1872 police warning about blocking the street while loading iron;
- an unrelated 1882 court settlement;
- an 1896 account extract;
- the Bär 1896 catalogue;
- the Schoch 1899 catalogue;
- a 1907 issue of *Schweizerische Bauzeitung*;
- a 1908–1918 envelope collection;
- later miscellaneous business records.

Primary finding aid:
- Stadtarchiv Zürich, `VII.382 Pestalozzi + Co AG, Firmenarchiv, 1788–1945`, p.7.
- https://amsquery.stadt-zuerich.ch/Dateien/21/D109662.pdf

## 1. Safe conclusion

What is secure:

> **The Pestalozzi/Wiser company archive preserves both a 1896 Bär & Co. catalogue and an 1899 Julius Schoch & Co. catalogue.**

This is useful evidence that catalogues from multiple Zürich iron merchants survive inside a third merchant firm's archival remains.

What is **not** secure from the finding aid:

- that the two catalogues were historically stored together;
- that they formed an original competitor-comparison file;
- that their current consecutive numbers reproduce a nineteenth-century filing order;
- that Pestalozzi/Wiser staff actively compared the two catalogues;
- that `4.4` represents a historical reference-library category rather than later archival grouping.

Because the parent class is `Verschiedenes`, modern adjacency has low evidentiary value for original use-context.

## 2. Revised value of the two retained catalogues

The retained objects remain highly valuable, but their value is **object-level**, not arrangement-level.

For both `4.4.7` Bär and `4.4.8` Schoch inspect/request:

`BINDING / OWNER MARK / OLD SHELFMARK / RECEIPT DATE / STAMP / ANNOTATION / PRICE MARK / PROFILE MARK / FINGERING-WEAR / INSERT / LETTER / ORDER REFERENCE / ADJACENT LOOSE MATERIAL / ARCHIVAL REHOUSING / ORIGINAL VS MODERN LABEL`.

Only those traces can show whether the catalogues functioned as working competitor/reference copies.

The especially important object-specific feature already recorded in the finding aid is that `4.4.7` includes **an extract from the Bauzeitung**. That conjunction may be historically meaningful, but even here determine physically whether the extract was originally inserted/attached or joined during later archival processing.

## 3. Consequence for the Schoch comparator

The Schoch catalogue remains a strong **functional comparator** because independent architectural evidence documents downstream use by profile number, geometry and dimensions.

It is **not strengthened into direct Bär-use evidence merely because the Pestalozzi archive preserves both firms' catalogues**.

Use three levels explicitly:

1. **SCHOCH FUNCTION CONTROL** — documented downstream use of numbered profiles;
2. **THIRD-PARTY CO-RETENTION** — Pestalozzi/Wiser archive contains both Bär and Schoch catalogues;
3. **ORIGINAL COMPARATIVE USE** — OPEN until material traces establish it.

## 4. Application-level methodological value

This correction reinforces a central rule of the project:

> **modern archival adjacency != historical documentary adjacency.**

The same caution must be applied to EBA 4. A modern `Profileisenalbum` description or consecutive archival unit does not by itself establish original album function, page order, publication genealogy or workflow relation. Physical carrier morphology and provenance traces are therefore not decorative details; they are required to reconstruct the information system.

## Resume point

Keep Bär and Schoch as **independent morphology/function controls** until object-level provenance demonstrates historical comparative use. Do not infer a competitor-reference packet from `VII.382, 4.4.7–4.4.8` numbering.

The OCR-only index treated much of the later British material as if it might continue the 1860 itinerary. Image control now proves that this is unsafe.

- OCR entry 315 / manuscript image explicitly carries an underlined `1862` above a comparative block containing Low Moor/Cumberland material and a numbered `9. Sheffield` section.

Do not collapse the 1860 study journey, the 1862 material, and the 1863 Krug journey.

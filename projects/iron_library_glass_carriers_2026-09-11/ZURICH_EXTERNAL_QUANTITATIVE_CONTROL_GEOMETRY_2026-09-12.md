# Zürich external quantitative control geometry for the Küderli/Bär core

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** PRE-VISIT EXTERNAL CONTROL / CORPUS GEOMETRY  
**Purpose:** define a small external comparison packet that can test whether changes observed onsite in Iron Library `EBA 4` are firm-specific or shared across the Zürich iron-trade network. This is **not** an instruction to turn the residency into a two-archive census.

## 0. Result

Stadtarchiv Zürich `VII.382 Pestalozzi + Co AG` preserves substantially more than the already identified conventions and 1879 profile-stock document. It provides an unusually clean quantitative/documentary control around the Iron Library Küderli sequence:

- inventories through **1866**;
- balance books / balances across **1868–86**;
- business expenditure through **1870**;
- convention/cartel and direct inter-firm correspondence involving **Schinz & Baer** in **1870–75** and **1879–85**;
- price-list sequences spanning **1853–87**;
- a **1879 Façon-Eisen stock-profile document**;
- later weight/dimension/load tables that show what a more explicitly quantified product-information regime eventually looked like.

This makes it possible to build an external comparison key without reading the entire Pestalozzi fonds.

Primary finding aid:
- Stadtarchiv Zürich, `VII.382 Pestalozzi + Co AG, Firmenarchiv, 1788–1945`: https://amsquery.stadt-zuerich.ch/Dateien/21/D109662.pdf

## 1. Quantitative accounting / stock controls

The finding aid lists a nearly serial run of inventories:

- inventories for 1788–1851 at short intervals;
- `Inventarium` **1854, 1856, 1858, 1860, 1862, 1864, 1866**;
- then a `Bilanz-Buch` **1868–72**;
- balances **1874, 1876, 1878, 1880, 1882, 1884, 1886**, continuing later.

Additional high-value item:

- `2.3.2` — compilation of receipts and expenditures of the Joh. David Wiser iron business for **1851–62**, explicitly including an **overview of goods received in 1862**.
- `2.3.4` — business expenditure book, **1837–70**.

### Use

Do not reconstruct the whole firm's finances. Extract only variables that help test the EBA 4 morphology:

`YEAR / STOCK_TOTAL / PRODUCT_CLASS / IRON_STEEL_SPLIT / PROFILE_OR_BAR_CATEGORY / SUPPLIER_ORIGIN_IF_PRESENT / VALUE / WEIGHT_OR_QUANTITY / UNIT / INVENTORY_CATEGORY_LABEL / NEW_OR_DISAPPEARING_CATEGORY`.

The most diagnostic years are:

- **1858–66** — baseline before the Schinz & Baer supply shift;
- **1868–72** — supply/coordination hinge;
- **1878–86** — profile/Normalprofil/transaction window.

### Claim ceiling

Balance sheets may be too aggregated to reveal product morphology. Their first use is to test whether product classes or stock values change; do not assume profile-level detail before inspection.

## 2. Network control — direct records with Schinz & Baer

The same fonds contains:

- `4.2.1` — correspondence on **Kartellabsprache** with Gebrüder Vogel, **Schinz und Baer**, Fäh und Schoch, **1870–73**;
- `4.2.2` — 1873 convention among Fäh & Schoch, **Schinz & Baer**, Gebrüder Vogel and Joh. David Wiser concerning sales `en détail und en gros`;
- `4.2.3` — convention correspondence, **1873–75**;
- `4.2.4` — chronologically arranged inter-firm business correspondence among Schinz & Baer, Gebrüder Vogel, Faeh/Schoch and J. D. Wiser, **1879–85**.

These are the strongest direct external controls because the Iron Library actor is named inside the other firm's archive.

### Minimal extraction schema

`DATE / SENDER / RECEIVER / PRODUCT / PRODUCT_IDENTIFIER / PRICE / DISCOUNT / CUSTOMER_CLASS / EN_GROS_OR_DETAIL / SUPPLIER / SOURCE_REGION / FREIGHT / DELIVERY / STOCK / QUANTITY / UNIT / SUBSTITUTE / EQUIVALENT / COMPETITOR / RULE_OR_CONVENTION_REFERENCE / SANCTION_OR_DISPUTE / PRICE_LIST_OR_PROFILE_REFERENCE`.

The key test is whether the same product identifiers/categories appear in Iron Library `/7`, `/14`, `/6`, `/4`, `/15`.

## 3. Price/profile control — near-perfect chronological overlap

Stadtarchiv Zürich lists:

- `5.1` — price lists and protocols, **1853–73**;
- `5.2` — price lists, **1869–75**;
- `5.3` — price lists, **1875–79**;
- `5.4` — `Profile des Façon-Eisen-Lagers der Eisenhandlung Joh. David Wiser in Zürich`, **1879**;
- `5.5` — price lists, **1879–87**.

Iron Library EBA 4 simultaneously preserves:

- `/14` Profileisenalbum **1877–85**, 3 vols;
- `/6` Preislisten **1878–80**, 2 files.

### Strongest controlled comparison

Compare only a small synchronized packet:

1. Wiser price list **1878/79**;
2. Wiser `Profile des Façon-Eisen-Lagers`, **1879**;
3. Wiser price list **1879/80**;
4. Schinz & Baer EBA 4/14 pages/layers dated or attributable to **1878–80**;
5. EBA 4/6 price lists **1878–80**.

This is a much stronger test of merchant commensurability than comparing EBA 4 to a generic engineering handbook.

### Compare

`PROFILE_FAMILY / DRAWING_STYLE / DIMENSION_ORDER / UNIT / WEIGHT / PROFILE_NUMBER / SUPPLIER_OR_MILL / PRICE / CURRENCY / STOCK_LANGUAGE / NORMALPROFIL_LANGUAGE / PAGE_ORDER / CROSS_REFERENCE`.

## 4. Later endpoint morphology — use only as endpoint, never back-projection

The Wiser/Pestalozzi fonds later contains:

- `5.11` — tables for calculating **weight of Stab- und Façon-Eisen**, **1892**;
- `5.15` — **weight and load-bearing table plus dimensional inventory list of iron and steel stock**, **1901**;
- `5.21` — dimensional, weight and load-bearing tables, **1908**;
- `5.23` — **Konventions-Preise pro 1912**, including a separate Zürich agreement;
- further general price lists divided by customer/technical classes in 1913.

### Analytical use

These later items prove that, in this merchant documentary ecology, the fields

`DIMENSION + WEIGHT + LOAD CAPACITY + STOCK + PRICE + CONVENTION RULE`

could eventually become explicitly articulated.

They do **not** prove that this integrated regime existed in 1879.

Use them to formulate a historical question:

> **when, and through which carriers, did dimensions, weight, engineering capacity, stock identity and collectively coordinated prices become linkable merchant information?**

The 1870s–80s material is the transition zone to test, not an assumed early version of the 1912 system.

## 5. Public-output controls for Schinz & Baer

Two external records give a late-1880s outward-facing endpoint for the Iron Library internal albums:

### 1889 advertisement

*Schweizerische Bauzeitung*, 25 May 1889, advertisement by `SCHINZ & BAER`, Zürich/Waaggasse/Eisenhalle:

- `Eiserne Tragbalken & Säulen`;
- `alle Bauartikel in Eisen und Guss`.

Source:
- https://www.e-periodica.ch/cntmng?pid=sbz-002%3A1889%3A13%3A%3A220

### 1889 illustrated stock prospectus

Staatsarchiv Thurgau catalogue:

- `Slg. 15, 8'4/105` — **Schinz und Bär, Eisenhandlung, Zürich und Basel: Faltprospekt zu ihrem Bauartikel-Lager mit Illustrationen zu den vorrätigen ...**, dated **1889**.

Catalogue context:
- https://query-staatsarchiv.tg.ch/archivplansuche.aspx?ID=344444

### Use

These are not central primary sources for the residency. They are a useful **output morphology** control:

`INTERNAL PROFILE / PRICE / STOCK ALBUM -> PUBLICLY PRESENTED BUILDING-IRON STOCK`.

If EBA 4/14 has product identifiers or image conventions that survive into the 1889 prospectus/advertising, this would establish an unusually clean carrier transformation.

## 6. 1885 Schinz & Baer patent — keep peripheral

A German technical notice reports that Schinz & Baer held `D.R.P. Nr. 34679`, 4 September **1885**, for an adjustable impact valve for hydraulic rams (`regulirbares Schlagventil für hydraulische Widder`).

Source:
- Dinglers Polytechnisches Journal, `Ueber Neuerungen an Pumpen`: https://dingler.bbaw.de/articles/ar264079.html

This also appears in the Italian patent index under `Societa Schinz e Baer`, 7 Dec 1885.

### Interpretation ceiling

This shows that the firm was not necessarily only a passive reseller, but it is currently **not linked to the profile-album/Convention problem**. Keep as peripheral firm-capability evidence; do not expand the application around it unless an EBA 4 document directly connects the patent to the merchant information system.

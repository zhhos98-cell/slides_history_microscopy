# Iron Library — original Berlin metallographic glass plates as a site-specific must-visit object cluster

Date: 2026-09-12

Status: HIGH-PRIORITY SITE-SPECIFIC HOLDING CONTROL. This file records a newly identified physical object cluster at the Eisenbibliothek that directly intersects the project's Berlin metallographic carrier history. It materially strengthens the case that the proposed research requires an on-site visit rather than only remote consultation of digitised books.

## 1. Direct holding statement

Bernhard Droste, longtime director/professor at the Bundesanstalt für Materialforschung und -prüfung (BAM), published in *Ferrum* 93 (2024):

**`Bücher für die Eisenbibliothek. Reflexionen über gespendete Bücher zur Bestandserweiterung der Bibliothek`, pp.128–135.**

The Eisenbibliothek's own issue page states that Droste donated **228 titles** from his private library, especially in materials science / material testing and related history-of-technology fields.

In the article itself Droste states that he was able to give the Eisenbibliothek:

- substantial annual volumes of the Berlin **`Mittheilungen aus den königlichen technischen Versuchsanstalten`** for **1889, 1892, 1894, 1895, 1896 and 1897**;
- **three original glass plates (`drei Originalglasplatten`) from these early microphotographic recordings**.

Source controls:
- Eisenbibliothek, *Ferrum* 93/2024 issue page / Droste abstract;
- Bernhard Droste article text as indexed by the GF Corporate Archive web object `GFA 30/239` / media object `94722`.

This establishes physical survival at the Eisenbibliothek of **original early metallographic photographic plates**, not merely printed reproductions.

## 2. The article's image caption identifies at least two plates more precisely

The Droste article contains a figure caption:

**`Glasplatten mit mikrofotografischen Aufnahmen der «königlichen mechanisch-technischen Versuchsanstalt zu Berlin»; mit Tafel der Veröffentlichung der Aufnahme unten links.`**

The figure is numbered `2` in the article's image credits and is photographed by the Eisenbibliothek under image credit / identifier:

**`Foto: Eisenbibliothek, G 745,1.`**

This is important for two reasons.

First, at least the plates shown in that figure are explicitly linked to the **Königliche mechanisch-technische Versuchsanstalt zu Berlin**.

Second, the article deliberately photographs the **glass plates together with the corresponding publication plate (`Tafel`) at lower left**.

The carrier relation is therefore physically demonstrable in the Eisenbibliothek:

**original photographic glass plate → published metallographic Tafel.**

This is almost exactly the transformation the current project has been reconstructing from Berlin annual reports and printed `Mittheilungen`.

## 3. Immediate bibliographic control beside the glass-plate figure

Droste's image credit no.1 identifies the adjacent Berlin publication as:

**Adolf Martens, `Die mikroskopische Ausrüstung der königlichen mechanisch-technischen Versuchsanstalt`, Mittheilungen aus den königlichen technischen Versuchsanstalten zu Berlin, 1891, pp.278–293, Tafeln IV–VI.**

Eisenbibliothek credit / shelf identifier:

**`Per765`.**

The article's figure no.2 is the photograph of the original glass plates (`G 745,1`).

This juxtaposition makes a direct Martens-1891 / plate correspondence a high-priority hypothesis to test physically, but it should not yet be stated as proven for all three plates. On-site comparison must establish exactly which negatives correspond to which `Tafel`, figure and publication year.

Use:

`MARTENS_1891_CORRESPONDENCE = HIGH-PRIORITY ONSITE TEST, NOT YET SECURE FOR ALL PLATES`.

## 4. Possible Cu/Cu2O plate — keep the claim ceiling strict

Immediately after mentioning the donation of three original plates, Droste writes that **a microstructural image from the binary Cu/Cu2O system is also reproduced in the donated book**:

**Emil Heyn and Oswald Bauer, `Metallographie`, I Allgemeiner Teil / II Spezieller Teil, Leipzig, 1913.**

This textual proximity makes it plausible that one of the original plates may be connected to that Cu/Cu2O image, but the article excerpt presently available does not justify identifying the third plate with certainty.

Therefore:

`Cu/Cu2O = POSSIBLE / TO BE VERIFIED AGAINST PHYSICAL PLATE + CAPTION / BOOK IMAGE`.

Do not upgrade it before direct object control.

## 5. Why the glass plates are not replaceable by online annual volumes

The research question is not simply `what image was published?`.

The original plates can preserve variables absent from a digital or printed reproduction:

- plate dimensions and format;
- glass thickness / edge form;
- emulsion side and coating extent;
- handwritten or printed labels;
- institutional marks / plate numbers / negative numbers;
- edge inscriptions;
- masking / cropping / retouching traces;
- orientation marks;
- damage / reuse / handling traces;
- relation between full negative field and cropped published Tafel;
- density / exposure differences lost in printed halftone or later scan;
- physical correspondence among negative, print, plate caption and publication figure.

These are exactly the variables needed to reconstruct **how a microscopic observation became a portable reference image**.

A digitised printed `Tafel` controls the published image. It does not substitute for the photographic carrier that generated it.

## 6. The Eisenbibliothek is a workable on-site research environment

The Eisenbibliothek's official `Services vor Ort` page states:

- library and GF corporate archive holdings are open for free scholarly/public use by appointment;
- the reading room at Klostergut Paradies is open Monday–Friday by prior arrangement;
- the Eisenbibliothek is a **reference library** whose holdings are consulted in the reading room;
- users may make scans and use their own camera;
- staff provide research consultation.

Therefore an object-level comparison of the original glass plates against the donated Berlin annual volumes is institutionally feasible as an on-site research operation.

Claim ceiling: the public site does not specifically state handling conditions for these three fragile plates. Their production to a reader, photography conditions and conservation restrictions must be confirmed with Eisenbibliothek staff before travel.

## 7. Why this creates a real `must visit` argument

The current project can now distinguish three evidence layers.

### A. Remotely recoverable publication layer

Digitised Berlin `Mittheilungen`, annual reports and technical literature allow reconstruction of:

- apparatus descriptions;
- preparation procedures;
- published Tafeln;
- annual counts / institutional activity;
- negative-archive and circulation systems.

### B. Eisenbibliothek physical carrier layer

The Eisenbibliothek uniquely holds, through the Droste donation:

- selected original Berlin annual volumes;
- **three original early metallographic glass plates**;
- at least one documented physical comparison between glass plate(s) and a publication Tafel in the Droste article.

### C. Research operation available only through object comparison

The visit can test:

**negative/plate → publication crop/Tafel → later book reproduction → archive/library survival.**

That is a carrier genealogy, not a bibliographic lookup.

The core necessity claim is therefore:

> **Remote sources can reconstruct what the Berlin institutions said and published; only the Eisenbibliothek's surviving glass plates can show how an original metallographic photographic carrier was physically transformed into the published and reusable visual reference object.**

This is a materially specific justification for travel.

## 8. Immediate pre-visit questions for Eisenbibliothek staff

Before finalising an application / visit plan, ask staff to confirm:

1. current internal identifiers / shelfmarks for the **three Droste original metallographic glass plates**;
2. whether `G 745,1` is only the modern photograph identifier or also points to the physical object record;
3. plate dimensions and conservation/handling status;
4. whether the plates bear original labels, negative numbers, annotations or envelopes;
5. exact provenance supplied by Droste / BAM;
6. which `Mittheilungen` volumes / Tafeln correspond to each plate;
7. whether the possible **Cu/Cu2O** image can be matched to one of the plates;
8. whether plates may be examined under transmitted light / photographed front-and-back / measured;
9. whether any original sleeves, notes, inventories or donation correspondence survive;
10. whether there are additional early metallographic negatives/plates in the donation beyond the three explicitly mentioned in the article.

## 10. Claim ceiling

Secure:

- Droste donated 228 titles to the Eisenbibliothek;
- his article explicitly says he gave the library Berlin `Mittheilungen` annual volumes 1889, 1892, 1894–97;
- he explicitly says he also donated **three original glass plates** from early microphotographic work;
- the article caption identifies glass plates with microphotographic images from the **Königliche mechanisch-technische Versuchsanstalt zu Berlin** and places a corresponding publication Tafel beside them;
- the article's image credit for that photograph is **Eisenbibliothek `G 745,1`**;
- Droste's adjacent bibliographic control identifies Martens' 1891 apparatus article, pp.278–293, Tafeln IV–VI, as Eisenbibliothek `Per765`;
- the Eisenbibliothek officially supports on-site scholarly use by appointment, permits user photography/scanning and is a reference library.

Strong inference:

- the glass plates are uniquely valuable for reconstructing the transformation from original metallographic negative to printed reference image;
- their material inspection supplies evidence unavailable from digitised `Mittheilungen` alone;
- this object cluster provides a defensible, collection-specific reason for an Iron Library research visit.

Open:

- exact identity/date/material of each of the three plates;
- exact plate-to-Tafel concordance;
- whether all three derive from Martens' mechanisch-technische work;
- whether one is the Cu/Cu2O image mentioned immediately afterwards;
- original BAM / testing-institute negative numbers and provenance chain;
- current shelfmark / handling status of the physical plates.

# Rauschenbach scale / retrieval correction

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** ACTIVE OVERRIDE FOR PUBLIC-CATALOG SCALE CLAIMS  
**Rule:** do not equate actor-linked breadth or long date envelopes with a proven large pre-1885 archival series.

## 0. Why this correction

Earlier collection-first maps promoted Rauschenbach as the strongest continuous pre-1890 industrial-company block in the GFA environment. That remains plausible as a **company/evidence cluster**, but the public ANTON metadata does **not** currently prove a Haffter- or Küderli-scale continuous pre-1885 archive.

The actor record exposes many Rauschenbach-related records, but much of the early-looking material is catalogued as one modern dossier with very broad chronology.

Official actor record:
- https://archives.georgfischer.com/actors/49

## 1. Publicly proven pre-1885 carrier core

### Strong serial carriers

- `GFA 1/806 Maschinenfabrik Rauschenbach, Mitarbeiterverzeichnisse`, 1871–1930 — **1 Band**.
- `GFA 1/809 Maschinenfabrik Rauschenbach, Krankenkasse`, 1872–1930 — **10 Bände**.

These eleven bound volumes are the clearest publicly demonstrated serial operational/labour carriers.

### Correspondence / operational dossiers

- `GFA 1/780 Rauschenbach: Korrespondenz, Verträge`, 1868–1905 — **1 Akte**.
- `GFA 1/781 Rauschenbach: Korrespondenz, Verträge`, 1869–1921 — **1 Akte**.
- `GFA 1/2288 Korrespondenzen J. Rauschenbach 1876–1879, Vereinbarungen Bucher-Guyern 1928–1941` — **1 Akte**.
- `GFA 1/786 Krise, Umzug auf Ebnat`, 1878–1934 — **1 Akte**.

Public metadata does not expose internal file counts or prove that these modern dossiers preserve continuous nineteenth-century subseries.

`/2288` demonstrates explicitly that one present dossier can join temporally unrelated nineteenth- and twentieth-century materials.

## 2. Broad-envelope dossiers are not evidence of early scale

Additional actor-linked records reinforce the caution:

- `GFA 1/782 Rauschenbach: Korrespondenz, Unterlagen`, **1800–1960** — 1 Akte;
- `/779 Entstehungsgeschichte, Liegenschaften, Verträge`, **1855–1921** — 1 Akte;
- `/2330 Läutemaschinen, Pläne Kräne Gotthardbahn, Schiffaufzug, SBB-Bremszylinder, Autoheber, Munitionsguss, Handgranatenguss, Sonnenenergie`, **1852–1981** — 1 Akte;
- `/2350 Holzbearbeitungsmaschinen ...`, **1842–1954** — 1 Akte;
- `/2255 Zeittafel Putzereimaschinen 1827–1968, Fotos Sandprüfung und -aufbereitung` — 1 Akte.

These records may contain important early originals, but their envelope dates and mixed titles show that they are retrospective/thematic containers rather than demonstrated continuous 1850s–80s archival runs.

## 3. Photographic layer

Contemporary or near-contemporary Rauschenbach imagery is real but separately carrier-organised:

- `GFA 1/141.161` old factory building, 1869 — 1 photo;
- `GFA 1/141.162` factory on the Rhine, 1880 — 1 photo;
- `GFA 16/12114` workshop, 1880–1885 — 1 photo;
- `GFA 13/95` building-development photo album, 1880–1950 — 1 album.

This supports a cross-carrier company cluster, not a large textual operational series by itself.

## 4. Formal governance remains later

`GFA 51 Maschinenfabrik Rauschenbach AG` has a fonds envelope beginning 1865, but the actual Generalversammlung / Verwaltungsrat protocol sequence starts with the **1892** conversion to an AG.

Do not use the GFA 51 envelope to inflate the pre-1885 company-archive scale.

The repository note for part II states that contemporary bibliographic reporting identifies it as a continuation and notes **the use of Winkel's marking apparatus and methods for selecting individual forms from mixed material**.

## Why this matters to the finder project

This is the first internal source route found in the present sweep whose technical vocabulary is directly adjacent to locating/marking an individual microscopic form rather than to cabinet addressing, series numbering or optical test standards.

It potentially intersects three operations that must be kept analytically distinct until the primary text is read:

1. **selection** — choosing an individual form from mixed material;
2. **marking** — making that selected form or its position materially recognizable;
3. **relocation** — returning later to the same form/position by a reproducible reference.

The current internal evidence establishes only that the bibliographic note associates Klein II with Winkel's marking apparatus and selection of individual forms. It does **not** yet establish which of the three operations the apparatus performed, its geometry, whether it acted on slide/coverslip/stage, or whether its mark travelled with the preparation.

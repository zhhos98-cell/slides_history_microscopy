# P1 source targets 1–2 — outcome, 2026-08-09

This note records the bounded source pass for the first two open primary-source targets. It does not alter the frozen 155-row object catalogue.

## 1. H. L. Smith — `Diatomacearum Species Typicae`

**Object node:** `US-FARLOW-HL-SMITH-DIATOMACEARUM-1876`

The journal identity is now resolved. The JRMS bibliography in `01B`, record `R22760` / document `D080`, p. 304, places `Notes on Century III. of the “Species Typicae Diatomacearum,” by Professor H. L. Smith, Geneva, N.Y.` under the **August 1878 contents of the _American Journal of Microscopy and Popular Science_**. The `American Quarterly Microscopical Journal` appears separately in the same bibliography and should not be conflated with this item.

A digitized copy of volume 3 (1878) is located at HathiTrust: `https://hdl.handle.net/2027/hvd.32044081507253`. The volume is therefore bibliographically located, but the Century III page images/text were not recoverable in this pass. Status: `LOCATED_VOLUME_TEXT_PENDING`.

A stronger same-series bridge was recovered from material already inside `01A`: _Monthly Microscopical Journal_ XVII (1877), pp. 100–101, records `R47249`–`R47250`, document `D135`. It reprints the January 1877 American notice on **Century I** and describes the first 100 slides, five pasteboard trays, numbered catalogue/labels, diamond-written slide numbers, the de Brébisson material, and contributions obtained by exchange or otherwise. This is now stored as `VERIFIED_DIRECT`. It must remain distinct from the unread August 1878 Century III article.

## 2. Charles Collins Jr. — fish scales

**Object node:** `UK-STANDREWS-CHARLES-COLLINS-SLIDES`

The exact missing notice is now bibliographically located as **_Microscopical News and Northern Microscopist_ IV (1884), p. 109, “Fish Scales.”** A source-specific transcription at `https://microscopist.net/CollinsCjr.html` reproduces the notice and identifies Charles Collins Jr. as the nephew of the microscope maker; it says he had just issued a series comprising **48 distinct species**.

This is consistent with the independent JRMS bibliography pointer already present in the corpus and with the direct 1884–1885 Collins evidence already held in _Science-Gossip_ and the _Journal of Microscopy and Natural Science_. A primary scan of volume IV was not recovered in this pass, so the new locator is classified `SOURCE_LOCATED_TRANSCRIPTION_PRIMARY_SCAN_PENDING`, not as primary-source ingestion.

## Disposition

Neither target warrants further open-ended discovery work now. Target 1 has the correct digitized volume located plus a direct Century I same-series bridge already in corpus. Target 2 has the exact p. 109 notice located and transcribed, with independent contemporary corroboration already in corpus. Primary page-image ingestion can be done later if convenient.

The next P1 target remains the **1881 Stazione Zoologica Napoli 16-page price catalogue**, which the St Andrews collection description records as listing 423 preparations.

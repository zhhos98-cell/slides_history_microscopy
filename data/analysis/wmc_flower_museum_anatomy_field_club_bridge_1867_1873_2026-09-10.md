## 1. Flower's professional museum/anatomy position

Royal College of Surgeons sources document William Henry Flower as Conservator of the Hunterian Museum from 1861 to 1884 and Hunterian Professor of Comparative Anatomy and Physiology from 1870 to 1873 (and again later). His earlier work also included anatomy teaching and museum curation at Middlesex Hospital.

## 2. Recurring WMC lectures before the 1873 presidency

The BNA WMC export supplies a longer WMC sequence than previously noted.

### 1867 — Human Anatomy

*Express (London)*, 23 Oct. 1867, `BL/0002642/18671023/031`, announces W. H. Flower, Conservator of the Royal College of Surgeons Museum, for the first of a course of lectures on `Human Anatomy` at the Working Men's College.

### 1870 — new rooms / public scientific programme

*Echo*, 6 Jan. 1870, `BL/0004596/18700106/014`, announces the WMC winter conversazione, the new class rooms being on view, and Flower as a forthcoming lecturer.

## 4. Why Flower matters to the operation-level argument

Flower carries a different package from Cooke, Grugeon or Lobley. His WMC appearances bring professional museum and comparative-anatomical expertise into several public/instructional settings before he appears as president of a body explicitly combining Museum study and open-air rambles.

This allows a precise question:

**What did the office of president contribute to a Society whose practical programme depended on Museum work, specimens and field study, and did Flower's museum expertise shape that programme?**

The current evidence establishes co-location, not causation. No controlled source yet states that Flower designed the Museum-work or specimen-mounting sessions.

## 5. A recurring pattern of specialist return / attachment

Flower should be compared with Seeley and Lobley:

- Flower: external professional museum/anatomy expert repeatedly lectures at WMC, then retrospectively appears as Society president;
- Seeley: former WMC student/Museum secretary develops professional geological expertise and returns as a Society lecturer;
- Lobley: WMC geology lecturer simultaneously edits the Geologists' Association *Proceedings*.

Together these are more informative than a flat `network`. They are distinct **interfaces** through which professional museum, geological and associational procedures become available to WMC students and clubs.

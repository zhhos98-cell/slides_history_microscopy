## 1. Lobley at WMC

The full BNA WMC export contains contemporary notices establishing J. Logan Lobley in WMC's geology teaching programme:

- *Globe*, 20 Oct. 1871, `BL/0001652/18711020/009`: announces that Lobley would deliver the introductory lecture to a geology course at the Working Men's College, 45 Great Ormond Street;
- *Morning Advertiser*, 20 Oct. 1871, `BL/0001427/18711020/056`: parallel notice naming J. Logan Lobley, F.G.S., F.R.G.S.;
- *Hour*, 27 Oct. 1873, `BL/0002814/18731027/004`: lists J. Logan Lobley speaking on `Geology` at WMC, 45 Great Ormond Street.

Thus Lobley's WMC connection spans at least 1871–1873 in contemporary public notices.

## 5. Documentary operation

Lobley's position is especially relevant to the project's operation-level approach:

- at WMC: geology is taught as a class / lecture sequence;
- in the Geologists' Association: field excursions expose sections, fossils and stratigraphy;
- in the *Proceedings*: those excursions are reformatted into named directors, routes, sections, observations, figures and references.

A person located across teaching and editorial machinery can potentially carry not only information but **formats of observation and documentation**.

No claim should be made that Lobley personally designed WMC pedagogy around the *Proceedings* until class-level evidence is recovered.

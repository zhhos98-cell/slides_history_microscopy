# WMC / M. C. Cooke / 1875 Museum curatorship claim — source audit

Updated: 2026-09-10
Status: `SMITH_2023_CLAIM_UNFOOTNOTED__WMC_PRIMARY_RECORD_OPEN__COOKE_EXTERNAL_CURATORIAL_STATUS_SECURE`
Scope: source-critical audit of the frequently useful but presently unclosed statement that M. C. Cooke was invited to become Curator of the Working Men's College Museum in 1875. This note supersedes any earlier wording that treated Smith 2023 as supplying an exact primary locator for the 1875 invitation.

## 1. Exact placement of the claim in Smith 2023

Nicholas E. C. Smith, `Fertile substrate: the rise, fall, and succession of popular microscopy in Great Britain`, *Annals of Science* 80.3 (2023), p. 274, writes that after leaving his projected WMC teaching course Cooke remained in contact with pupils including Alfred Grugeon and remained known to the College, `being invited to become the Curator of its Museum in 1875`.

The footnote structure matters:

- note 26 belongs to the preceding biographical / educational context;
- note 27 is attached to the sentence on Cooke's 1861 WMC Physiological Botany teaching and cites `Report of General Meeting`, *Working Men's College Magazine* 3 (1861), p. 173;
- the 1875 curator-invitation sentence itself carries **no dedicated note**;
- note 28 appears with the following Society of Amateur Botanists discussion and refers to the Society's proceedings.

Therefore the 1875 statement is **not** an exact-source locator supplied by Smith.

Revised status:

`COOKE_INVITED_WMC_MUSEUM_CURATOR_1875 = MODERN_SECONDARY_UNFOOTNOTED_CLAIM__PRIMARY_SOURCE_REQUIRED`.

Do not cite Smith's footnote 27 as evidence for the 1875 event.

## 2. The claim remains historically plausible but plausibility is not closure

Independent institutional / scholarly sources establish that Cooke was already doing professional museum-curatorial work by the mid-1870s:

- Royal Botanic Gardens Kew's J. D. Hooker correspondence apparatus describes Cooke as curator at the India Museum / India Office from the 1860s until the 1879 transfer of botanical material to Kew;
- Royal Holloway's Mobile Museum project states that Cooke joined the India Museum after his 1862 exhibition/cataloguing work and became assistant curator in 1874;
- later India Office/Kew arrangements explicitly carried both botanical collections and Cooke's curatorial services into Kew.

Thus an 1875 WMC invitation, if verified, would involve WMC approaching someone with current professional collection experience. It cannot presently be assumed that he accepted the invitation, served in the office, or transferred India Museum practices/objects into WMC.

Do not infer:

- `Cooke was WMC Museum Curator in 1875`;
- `Cooke left the India Museum for WMC`;
- `Cooke curated the same WMC collection documented in 1860 or 1874`;
- `Cooke introduced a professional museum system at WMC`.

## 3. Why the primary WMC record matters unusually much

By 1874 the BNA corpus independently controls scheduled `Museum work, Mounting Specimens, &c.` inside the WMC Natural History Society and Field Club. Ramsbottom later identifies Grugeon as a WMC Museum curator. If a 1875 invitation to Cooke is confirmed, it could place a named curatorial office immediately after a period of active museum/specimen work and could clarify whether the Museum itself, rather than only a club room or temporary display, carried natural-history material across institutional forms.

The exact document needs to answer at least:

1. who issued the invitation: Council, Executive Committee, club, Museum committee, or an individual;
2. whether the office was honorary, paid or attached to other teaching/service;
3. whether Cooke accepted;
4. what collection or room was described;
5. whether duties included acquisition, classification, preservation, teaching, display or supervision of student labour;
6. whether Grugeon or another later curator is named in succession.

## 4. Exact archive routing after AIM25 collection-level control

The London Archives / AIM25 collection description for `LMA/4535` directly confirms that the WMC archive contains:

- Directors Minutes, 1857–1878;
- Council Minutes, 1854–1997;
- Executive Committee Minutes, 1863–1989;
- College ledgers, 1858–1905;
- significant records of student clubs;
- Great Ormond Street building plans among later architectural material.

This makes the 1875 invitation a bounded minute/committee search rather than a generic archive request.

Priority physical/digital request:

`1875 Directors + Council + Executive Committee minutes: Cooke / Museum / curator / natural history / specimens / Natural History Society / Field Club / Grugeon`.

Search several months on either side of any exact date if one is recovered from English 1987 or Smith's notes.

## Evidence/status

This attachment is an **archive-routing source**, not yet direct evidence of slide transport, postal circulation, breakage, packing, loan, or remounting.

What it closes securely:

- HSM holds a substantial body of RMS internal institutional records in addition to the separately listed `MSS Royal Microscopical Society` manuscripts.
- The internal archive includes minute runs covering the project's core nineteenth-century decades.
- The internal archive includes at least one explicitly described **`Register of slides 1897`**.
- The material is off-site and requires at least one week's advance ordering.

What remains open:

- whether the nineteenth-century minutes contain slide-loan, postal-circulation, packing, breakage, repair, replacement, cabinet, or exchange entries;
- whether the 1897 slide register describes the same physical cabinet later deposited at HSM;
- whether any historic register/cabinet address can be cross-walked to a current `Small Worlds` container/drawer/slide/specimen address;
- whether account/ledger material contains postage, carriage, packing, box, insurance, or other logistics costs.

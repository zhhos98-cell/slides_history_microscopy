# Omission policy

Pages publishes the backend's compact metadata and research-result layers. Large canonical payloads stay out when duplication would mainly add weight: page-level OCR/full text, extension full-text bodies, structured-OCR page arrays, BNA article payloads/raw OCR, and large BNA record-level derived tables. Their source-master counts and SHA-256 fingerprints remain published in `CORPUS_MANIFEST_V4.json`.

# Slide-locked bibliography pass 11 — German-speaking trade, production and teaching module

Date: 2026-08-10

This pass adds 14 verified records (12 primary, 2 research) under the unchanged physical-slide-first admission rule.

Core closures:

- serial commercial states of J. D. Möller's preparation business: 1877, 1883, 1889 and 1897 price lists, extending the already-listed 1868 catalogue;
- Eduard Kaiser's 1877 Institut für Mikroskopie advertisement linked to surviving address-labelled slides;
- Klönne & Müller from the 1879 `Duncker'sche` preparations advertisement to the 1885 specialist catalogue of single, collective, test, type and salon preparations;
- Otto Bachmann's 1880 Landsberg catalogue as a unusually detailed source for school teaching, slide formats, labels, protective cartons, approval shipments, replacements and prices;
- Ludwig Klein's paired 1888 technical papers on freshwater-algae permanent preparations;
- Möller's 1891 phototype atlas and 1892 species index as a physical-preparation → image-publication → textual-index chain;
- modern object studies of Hermann Welcker's rediscovered Halle preparations and Virchow-associated Trichinella slides at the Charité.

Instrument-first catalogues and broad anatomical catalogues remain excluded when microscopic preparations are incidental.

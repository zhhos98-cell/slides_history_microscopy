Research sources

Where the slides
leave records.

A research index to archives, manuscript fonds, society records, trade archives, historical slide collections, institutional catalogues, and digital primary-source portals. The aim is to make the route from an object to its documentary world visible without turning a useful lead into a provenance claim.

Source rule

A source route is not an object identity.

A museum can hold a nineteenth-century slide without preserving its original purchase record. An archive can preserve a maker's correspondence without proving that a particular surviving slide passed through that archive's institution. Current custody, historical ownership, manufacture, distribution, use, and later cataloguing therefore remain separate relations until a source connects them.

The registry favours holdings that can move research from a broad name hit toward a recoverable document, cabinet, register, label, catalogue, letter, account, or object record. Aggregate collection counts are retained only as descriptions of repositories; they are never converted into historical slide totals.
